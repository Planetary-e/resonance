# DEC-013: Authorize physical power measurement with sudo powermetrics

## Context and verified evidence

The owner explicitly authorized `sudo powermetrics` and completed a manual sample on the Mac15,6 host. The sample reports CPU/GPU/ANE power in milliwatts and confirms that the instrument works. This agent's shell cannot reuse the interactive sudo ticket, so a user-invoked privileged wrapper is required.

## Decision

Authorize collection of physical power samples for the approved RSO5 laboratory scenarios and baseline windows using `sudo powermetrics`, with sanitized evidence and no other privileged actions.

## Boundaries

- Record actual timestamped power samples, units and missing/censored samples; never convert CPU time or work bytes into energy.
- Preserve run identities, source/config hashes, timing, warmup/cooldowns and failure history. No scenario, seed or analysis changes after seeing results.
- Join baseline and recovery power to process/network/work artifacts without double counting. Report absolute and baseline-subtracted energy only where a matched valid baseline exists.
- No production, K/R, economic or budget approval follows from power availability. Prices, value and affordability remain separate owner decisions.
- No host serial/UUID, IP address, private key, plaintext, credential or private log may enter delivery artifacts.

## Impact on PRD/ARCH/AI/CMP/EVL/FEAT IDs

FEAT-001 remains blocked for full acceptance. This enables only the physical-power portion of E2; E3, economic affordability, production and K1–K8 remain open.

## Resolution, source, and approval evidence

Approved by the owner's instruction “Let's allow sudo power metrics instead” and the completed sample in `delivery/results/econ-power-sampler-check.txt`.
