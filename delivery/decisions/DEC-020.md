# DEC-020: Explore client-held content and leased discovery indexes

## Context

The owner proposed a different product/storage direction: original content remains on the client; a local model derives a summary/intent index; existing embedding/hashing/matching infrastructure is reused; the network stores and replicates only the leased discovery index; clients prove availability and reproduce missing indexes; original content is supplied by the online client after authorization. A bounded encrypted network mailbox may hold requests while the recipient is offline.

The owner authorized this exploration and creation of a dedicated branch. This is a product-scope architecture exploration, not approval to replace the current production recovery path or claim existing privacy/quality gates pass.

## Decision

Authorize one architecture and feasibility exploration covering:

1. Client-held originals, local summary/intent generation, signed versioned discovery indexes and exact idempotent republication.
2. Existing local embedding, hashing, matching, admission, replication and private-delivery reuse.
3. Index leases, availability proofs, refresh/expiry, updates, tombstones and client responsibility.
4. Bounded encrypted offline-request mailboxes with quotas, acknowledgment, deduplication and expiry.
5. Privacy, multilingual quality, malicious-index, rollback, client-offline/loss, resource and operational implications.
6. A local generative-model benchmark plan, separating summary/intent generation from the existing embedding model and deterministic hashing.

Produce one coherent proposed architecture, an executable evaluation plan and an independent review. Identify exactly which existing product/evidence/recovery requirements would change and need separate owner approval.

## Boundaries

- Design and authoritative research only; no product implementation, dependency installation, model download, product tests, campaign, deployment, installer or release.
- Do not claim that a local LLM perfectly understands intent, that summaries/embeddings are automatically confidential, or that signatures prove content truth.
- Preserve authorization, consent, current-policy, conflict/denial/retry and audit evidence unless the owner later approves a concrete scope change.
- No original plaintext or private contact address enters a public index. No unsalted original-content hash becomes a confirmation oracle without review.
- Offline unavailability and client-loss behavior must be explicit; no hidden always-on service or permanent network content archive.
- One design pass and one independent review. Implementation requires a separate owner decision.

## Impact

This exploration is separate from FEAT-001's rejected production recovery architectures. FEAT-001 and all R/K gates remain open; product/AI/privacy/compliance/release approvals require explicit review of any proposed scope change.
