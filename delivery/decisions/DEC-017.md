# DEC-017: Explore stateless production recovery architecture

## Context

RSO7 global semantic state and RSO8-Range32 sharding are independently confirmed rejected under original physical/work contracts. The owner selected exploration of sparse/stateless semantic verification. This is a new architecture line, not an RSO7/RSO8 revision.

## Decision

Authorize one RSO9 design pass using compact authenticated state roots, command-specific inclusion/non-inclusion proofs and on-demand original evidence, with established authenticated-data-structure references and one independent review. Produce at most one detailed design with exact verifier state, proof schemas, transition/certification rules, evidence placement, recovery and physical/work forecasts.

## Hypothesis and falsifiers

Hypothesis: verifiers can retain constant or logarithmically bounded semantic state rather than all accepted rows, while command-specific authenticated witnesses reproduce exact original reducer outcomes and original evidence remains available for recovery.

Reject or revise if any command needs unbounded/full-state witnesses; inclusion and non-inclusion are not authoritative; denials/conflicts/splits/retries cannot be reproduced; evidence retention is hidden or exceeds physical limits; configuration continuity fails; or proof/recovery work exceeds original limits.

## Boundaries

- Design and primary-source research only; no implementation, dependency installation, product tests, campaign, installer, deployment or release.
- Preserve exact original business objects/signatures/reducers/rights/denials/conflicts/uncertainty, five/quorum-four/one-Byzantine trust, four-copy availability, role privacy and complete prospective funding.
- Preserve original C/L/D/P/F/W/cumulative/aggregate/metadata and 3 s/30 s limits. DEC-012 is research-only.
- Account for every original 10,000-row evidence object and all four copies, semantic witnesses, non-inclusion proofs, pending/current roots, indexes, configuration ancestry, failed extents and observer/policy/network work. No unnamed storage or per-participant cumulative reset.
- Do not introduce a trusted recent root without original authorization continuity, probabilistic conflict semantics, replica plaintext or a new cryptographic dependency without separate approval.
- One design pass and one independent review. Implementation requires a separate owner decision.

## Impact

Reopens FEAT-001 for stateless design exploration only. FEAT-002–FEAT-011 and all R/K gates/approvals remain not ready/open/pending.
