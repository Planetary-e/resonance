# DEC-001: Preserve recovery suspension or authorize a genuinely different model review

## Context and verified evidence

R1/R2 and every K1–K8 gate remain open. RSO3 and RSO4 are rejected and implementation is suspended under `AGENTS.md`, `HANDOVER.md`, and `docs/developers/v0.4-completion-plan.md`.

RSO4's final independent design review requires 299,703 bytes against C=262,144, and even its reduced model requires 268,471 bytes. The required pending-generation protocol is fundamental to crash-safe four-copy availability. The rejected designs must not be tuned, thresholds must not be changed, and stopped campaigns must not be rerun.

A separate finite experimental slice has focused/composed test and independent review acceptance, but that evidence does not close R1/R2 or any K1–K8 gate.

## Question or proposed change

After the five contracts and evaluation library are generated, should this session:

1. preserve the suspension and stop at the existing owner gate; or
2. authorize one bounded, genuinely different recovery-model design review?

The owner is not being asked to approve a release, change a KPI/trust/resource target, or authorize implementation.

## Options and recommendation

- **Option 1 — preserve suspension:** finish onboarding and leave the next action at the owner gate. This is the default recommendation because no genuinely different model has yet been selected or independently reviewed.
- **Option 2 — authorize design review only:** permit one bounded comparison/design exercise for a fundamentally different model. It must address the RSO4 review questions, established alternatives, complete physical closure, failure states, and unchanged acceptance limits. Independent review and a separate owner decision are required before implementation or any campaign.

Continuing RSO3/RSO4, inferring a larger threshold, or rerunning a stopped campaign is not an available option.

## Impact on PRD/ARCH/AI/CMP/EVL/FEAT IDs

The decision determines FEAT-001 and the saved `next_action` for the B-HISTORY-LIFECYCLE / K2–K3 line. Downstream R5 integration and R6 qualification remain blocked regardless of the choice.

## What this blocks and what may proceed

Blocks all recovery implementation, production integration, and campaign execution. Does not block read-only inventory, the five contracts, evaluation definitions, or preservation of existing evidence.

## Resolution, source, and approval evidence

**Resolved: Option 1 — Keep suspension.** The product owner explicitly selected this option at 2026-10-25T19:10:50Z. Onboarding artifacts may be completed, but no genuinely different recovery-model design review, recovery implementation, stopped campaign, threshold/trust change, production integration or release is authorized.

FEAT-001 remains `blocked`; the project finishes onboarding as `paused`. A future owner decision is required to authorize a genuinely different design review or continue suspension. This resolution does not approve software architecture, AI architecture, compliance, any K1–K8 gate or production release.
