# DEC-018: Derive an evidence-based production resource rebaseline

## Context

The owner selected “Rebase production requirements” after RSO7/RSO8/RSO9 were independently rejected. A legal 10,000-row accept-generated history requires at least 10,240,000 B of signed evidence per copy before encoding, against C=262,144 B. Prover indexes exceed D, issuer-wide transitions exceed P/F/W, and recovery/network forecasts exceed current limits. Routine representation alternatives are closed.

## Decision

Authorize one bounded production resource-contract derivation and independent review. Propose explicit C/L/P/D/F, per-operation and cumulative work, aggregate storage/entries/metadata, network and deadline requirements derived from the legal 10,000-row workload and complete four-copy recovery obligations. Include uncertainty margins and operational/economic consequences.

The derived values are a proposal for a subsequent owner approval, not automatically accepted requirements. No old failure is relabeled and no candidate passes under old gates retroactively.

## Required analysis

- Start from actual original signed objects and exact reducer semantics, not a reduced fixture or deduplicated fiction.
- Include four evidence copies, original requests/votes/certificates, delegations, pending/current state, prover indexes, configuration ancestry, failed extents, retries, observer/policy operations and recovery.
- Derive legal issuer-wide attribution/retirement and hot-key workloads, not only balanced ordinary operations.
- Show minimum lower bounds, recommended allowances, headroom and sensitivity separately. Do not invent safety factors as measurements.
- Preserve aggregate and per-node accounting; explain network, memory, power, labor and volunteer-availability consequences without inventing evidence.
- Preserve five/quorum-four/one-Byzantine trust, exact original business semantics, role privacy and prospective funding.
- Identify any requirement that cannot be responsibly rebaselined without changing the product's purpose.

## Boundaries

Analysis and independent review only: no implementation, product tests, campaign, dependency installation, production routing, installer, deployment or release. No stopped campaign rerun. Existing K/R acceptance remains open and the new contract requires separate owner approval.

## Impact

Reopens FEAT-001 only for production resource-contract analysis. Other features and approvals remain not ready/open/pending.
