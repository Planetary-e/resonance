# DEC-005: Authorize RSO6 incremental-recovery design review

## Context and verified evidence

The owner selected the recommended incremental recovery architecture after reviewing the accepted RSO5 cost analysis. Actual RSO5 work is 677,973,227 B, driven principally by full-state replication, historical evidence reconstruction and recovery delegation/payload amplification. No alternative has established cap compliance, and R1/R2 and K1–K8 remain open.

## Decision

The owner approved Option 1: one bounded RSO6 design and bottom-up resource forecast for incremental recovery, followed by independent review. This authorizes design work only, not implementation.

## Required design direction

- Signed incremental control deltas with full state snapshots only at cuts and a bounded suffix.
- Crash-safe snapshot/compaction and pending-generation funding without RSO4's mixed-generation availability failure.
- Signed-bytes binary transport without JSON/base64 reconstruction; every real copy remains charged.
- Authenticated incremental evidence/activation indexes with exact original-reducer outcomes and single-pass transition construction.
- Four-status/one-payload recovery with exact digest-race retry and preflighted durable delegation restoration.

Preserve exact original objects/signatures/reducers/rights/denials, role privacy, five pinned members, quorum four and at most one Byzantine, four-copy availability, physical/work/deadline limits and prospective funding before promises. Do not tune RSO5, rerun stopped campaigns or infer a larger cap.

## Budget and gates

One coherent design/forecast pass, one independent review and at most one documentation correction for material findings. No production edits, product tests, implementation, campaign, installer, Git/CI or release action. The design stops if honest C/L/D/P/F or work closure fails.
