# DEC-016: Explore sharded production recovery architecture

## Context

RSO7 is independently confirmed rejected because one legal 10,000-row semantic generation exceeds the unchanged D partition even before current+pending obligations. The owner selected exploration of sharded state rather than changing resource limits or stopping recovery development. This is a new architecture line, not another RSO7 revision.

## Decision

Authorize one design pass for RSO8, a sharded authenticated state/recovery architecture, with established sharded-BFT primary-source comparison and one independent review. Produce at most one detailed design with exact shard ownership, command routing, cross-shard semantics, durable state cuts, configuration continuity, private evidence recovery and physical/work forecasts.

## Hypothesis and falsifiers

Hypothesis: partitioning semantic rows by an authoritative business decision key can bound every node's current+pending D state while retaining four-copy durability and Byzantine agreement. A legal 10,000-row workload must not require every node to retain the complete semantic state.

Reject or revise the candidate if it merely replicates global state, cannot route every conflict/denial/retry to authoritative shards, permits unsafe cross-shard acceptance, loses original evidence/rights, fails configuration continuity or four-copy recovery, or cannot fit original physical/work/deadline requirements.

## Boundaries

- Design and authoritative public research only; no implementation, dependency installation, product tests, campaign, installer, deployment or release.
- Preserve exact original business objects/signatures/reducers/rights/denials/conflicts/uncertainty and role-private state; original evidence remains available and charged.
- Preserve five pinned members, quorum four and at most one Byzantine per authorized shard; four independently durable copies; original-genesis and configuration continuity; complete prospective funding.
- Preserve original C/L/D/P/F/W/cumulative/aggregate/metadata and 3 s/30 s requirements. DEC-012 remains research-only.
- Include every shard, global/configuration metadata object, cross-shard certificate, current+pending state, failed extent, observer/policy operation, network path and recovery cost in the model.
- Do not reduce accepted-row capacity, prune originals, create an unaccounted global index, hide state in another partition or invoke per-shard resets as a cumulative-work shortcut.
- One design pass and one independent review. Implementation requires a separate owner decision.

## Impact

Reopens FEAT-001 for sharded design exploration only. FEAT-002–FEAT-011 and all R/K gates/approvals remain not ready/open/pending.
