# DEC-006: Explore an established BFT recovery architecture

## Context and verified evidence

The owner requested a different approach after RSO6 was rejected before implementation. RSO3/RSO4/RSO5 implementations and forecasts failed physical or work limits; RSO6's optimistic forecast also exceeded them. The accepted cost analysis identifies full-state replication and historical reconstruction as central problems. R1/R2 and K1–K8 remain open.

## Decision

Authorize one bounded exploration of a genuinely different recovery approach based on an established Byzantine-fault-tolerant replicated state machine and append-only evidence storage rather than another custom RSO representation. Compare suitable established methods, select at most one for a concrete closure forecast, and obtain independent review.

## Boundaries

- Design and authoritative public research only; no implementation, dependency installation, product tests, campaign, Git/CI or release action.
- Preserve five pinned members, quorum four and at most one Byzantine; exact original business objects/signatures/rights/denials and role-private state.
- Do not substitute crash-only Raft or weaken Byzantine authentication, original evidence, four-copy availability or prospective physical funding.
- Evaluate integration language/runtime cost, operational complexity and dependency/security provenance honestly.
- Preserve physical/work/deadline limits and include all mandatory policy/observer, network and recovery costs in forecasts.
- One design pass, one independent review and at most one documentation correction. Implementation requires a separate owner decision.

## Impact on PRD/ARCH/AI/CMP/EVL/FEAT IDs

Reopens FEAT-001 for design exploration only. FEAT-002–FEAT-011 remain `not_ready`. EVL-003/004/005/015 and all K gates remain open.
