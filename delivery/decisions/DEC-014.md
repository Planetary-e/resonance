# DEC-014: Explore production recovery architecture alternatives

## Context and verified evidence

The owner explicitly authorized continuing the production recovery feature and finding alternatives. RSO5 is accepted only as a bounded research prototype; original production caps and failures remain preserved. RSO5/RSO6 and the CometBFT sidecar were rejected for production because repeated reconstruction, full-state replication, recovery amplification or sidecar obligations exceeded the original work limits. DEC-013 later supplied partial physical diagnostics and an unclassified sustained failure; it does not authorize a rerun or establish economic viability.

## Decision

Authorize one production-architecture design exploration comparing at most two genuinely different candidates:

1. A single authenticated Byzantine state-machine path that evaluates each command once, votes on compact transition certificates, and uses certified state cuts plus bounded suffixes instead of reconstructing/replicating a full journal in every agreement phase.
2. Full adoption of an established BFT engine as the application runtime, rather than the rejected CometBFT Node sidecar, only if its semantics, integration and resource model are credible.

Produce a comparison and at most one recommended design with a bottom-up physical/work closure forecast and executable fault-schedule plan. Obtain one independent architecture review. No candidate is accepted merely because its forecast fits.

## Hypothesis and falsifiers

The first candidate's hypothesis is that one deterministic evaluation per committed command plus compact votes and incremental evidence can remove the measured agreement-phase reconstruction cost without changing business semantics. It is falsified by incomplete original-reducer equivalence, an unsafe cut/fault schedule, physical overflow, or per-operation/cumulative forecasts above the original limits.

The established-engine candidate is falsified by inability to preserve exact original semantics/privacy/configuration continuity, an unsafe or unavailable state-transfer model, unbounded runtime/resource obligations, or a forecast above the original limits.

## Boundaries

- Design and authoritative public research only; no implementation, dependency installation, product tests, campaign, installer, deployment or release action.
- Preserve five pinned members, quorum four and at most one Byzantine; exact original business objects/signatures/reducers/rights/denials/uncertainty; role-private state; four-copy availability; and complete prospective funding.
- Preserve original C=262,144 B, L/P=65,536 B, D=524,288 B, F=32,768 B, operation W=4,718,592 B, cumulative 268,435,456 B, aggregate 2 GiB/512 entries/1 MiB metadata and 3 s/30 s deadlines for production evaluation. DEC-012 remains research-only and is not a production budget.
- Include all policy/observer, network, crash, recovery, pending-state and failed-extent work in forecasts. No omitted phase, per-participant reset, replica plaintext or trusted recent snapshot without authorization continuity.
- Do not rerun stopped campaigns or tune RSO3–RSO6/CometBFT constants. Historical alternatives and effort are not reset.
- One comparison pass and one independent review. Implementation requires a separate owner decision after the review.

## Impact on PRD/ARCH/AI/CMP/EVL/FEAT IDs

Reopens FEAT-001 for production design exploration only. FEAT-002–FEAT-011 remain not ready; all R/K gates and software/AI/compliance/release approvals remain open or pending.
