# DEC-024: Application memory limit of 2 GiB

## Context

The client-held discovery candidate has three-run nominal and burst campaigns with all 3,006 operations good in each campaign. The existing application peak-memory comparison used 1.5 GiB. The final burst campaign peaked at 2,141,306,880 B (1.994 GiB), below the separate 2 GiB startup envelope but above the 1.5 GiB comparison. Attribution found no duplicate retained state and supports allocator/RSS retention and GC timing variability rather than a demonstrated proportional live-object leak.

## Decision

The owner explicitly approved changing the application resident-memory constraint from **1.5 GiB to 2 GiB**, stating: "Let's change the Memory constrains to 2GiB instead of 1.5GiB I am fine with that number."

Apply 2 GiB to both sustained and startup application-memory comparisons. Preserve the model-free per-relay limit of 512 MiB, all latency/operation/network/power/privacy/deployment gates, and all historical 1.5 GiB failures as failures under the old target. Do not relabel history.

## Limits and remaining risk

- The highest observed burst peak passes the new limit by only **5.89 MiB** (6,176,768 B). This is thin headroom, not a demonstration of stable operation under every workload or host.
- No forced GC, suppressed measurement, workload reduction or additional target change is authorized.
- Physical power, independent-host/impaired-network evidence, K2/K3 recovery, K4 fairness, K5/K6 privacy and K8 independent deployment remain open.
- This decision does not constitute production, K/R or release acceptance.

## Implementation evidence

`packages/eval/src/client-held-local-resources.ts` sets `maximumApplicationRssBytes` to 2 GiB. `docs/developers/v0.4-acceptance-kpis.md` records the same K7 target and preserves historical results. Current blocker reporting retains the thin-margin warning and all unrelated gates.
