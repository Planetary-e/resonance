# DEC-003: Authorize bounded RSO5 implementation measurement

## Context and verified evidence

DEC-002 produced the RSO5 append-only frame/checkpoint design. Independent R1 review confirmed its physical C closure and per-operation forecasts but rejected its four-node cumulative-work forecast of 270,132,258 B against 268,435,456 B, an excess of 1,696,802 B. This was a forecast, not a measured implementation result.

## Decision

The owner explicitly instructed “Do the test and let's if that calculation is accurate.” This authorizes one bounded experimental RSO5 implementation and diagnostic measurement of the approved plan, including the complete four-node/two-transition operation inventory needed to compare actual work with the forecast.

## Scope and limits

- Preserve exact originals, business signatures/reducers, rights, denials, role privacy, five pinned members, quorum four and at most one Byzantine.
- Preserve physical C/L/D/P/F, aggregate storage/metadata, deadlines and the 4,718,592-byte per-operation work limit.
- Cumulative collection may continue beyond 256 MiB solely to measure the actual total and first crossing; this is not acceptance or a threshold change.
- Use a parallel fresh-format experimental path, not production routing or an RSO2/RSO3 migration.
- Run focused safety/correctness checks and actual-source noEmit before the lifecycle measurement.
- Record actual runtime calls rather than manufacturing forecast counts. Reconcile the forecast's 120 full phase evaluations with 102 actual calls and two view-change groups with eight member calls.
- One independent protocol/privacy/resource review and correctness/metering corrections are allowed. No work-tuning round, 100-transition campaign, production integration, installer, Git/CI or release action is authorized.

## Impact on PRD/ARCH/AI/CMP/EVL/FEAT IDs
