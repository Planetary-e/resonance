# DEC-004: Remove the work ceiling for the RSO5 diagnostic measurement

## Context and verified evidence

DEC-003 preserved the per-operation work cap during the diagnostic. That prevented bootstrap and made the complete cumulative forecast unmeasurable. The owner correctly identified that a measurement intended to determine actual excess cannot enforce the threshold whose exceedance is being measured.

## Decision

The owner explicitly instructed: “Do not impose a budget! How do you pretend you are going to calculate the excess if you do not let it grow beyond your cap?”

This authorizes a complete RSO5 diagnostic with **no work-meter ceiling**. There must be no substitute 256 MiB, 512 MiB or other diagnostic work cap. Instrumentation records actual work and reports the original 4,718,592-byte operation and 268,435,456-byte cumulative thresholds only as comparison values.

## Scope and limits

- Preserve physical storage, original business objects/signatures/reducers, rights, denials, role privacy, trust/quorum and safety controls.
- Do not change the acceptance contract or call the uncapped run a pass. Actual threshold crossings are evidence, not approval.
- Complete the intended four-node/two-transition inventory, including actual phase calls, retries, member newView calls and full local-C-loss recoveries, unless a safety/correctness defect blocks the scenario.
- Record per-operation and cumulative actuals, first crossings and forecast differences without normalization or extrapolation.
- Correct only reachable correctness or metering defects found in implementation/review; do not optimize work to obtain a preferred result.
- No 100-transition campaign, production integration, installer, Git/CI or release action is authorized.

## Impact on PRD/ARCH/AI/CMP/EVL/FEAT IDs

Reopens FEAT-001 only for the uncapped measurement. FEAT-002–FEAT-011 remain `not_ready`; all K1–K8 and architecture/compliance/release approvals remain open or pending. The result will not accept RSO5 or close EVL-003/004/005/015.
