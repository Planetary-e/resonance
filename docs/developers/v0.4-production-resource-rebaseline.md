# DEC-018 production resource rebaseline

**Status: REVISE — independent review does not approve this contract.** All values below are unapproved costing proposals. Actual production approval requires a product/deployment-direction decision, a complete contract revision and missing operational evidence. No old failure becomes a pass, and no existing candidate is accepted retroactively. See `../evals/v04-dec018-resource-rebaseline-review-2026-11-21.md`.

## Recommendation and decision boundary

The report recommends submitting the following values for independent review as a derived production-envelope proposal, not as an acceptance-ready rebaseline. They demonstrate that preserving the legal 10,000-row workload, all original evidence, four copies, issuer-wide transitions, and 100 configurations requires a materially larger volunteer deployment.

A responsible production approval is **not** possible under the existing lightweight-volunteer assumptions without further evidence. In particular, the existing 512 MiB relay-memory target, 1 MiB/completion network target, and unmeasured power/labor/availability costs cannot be retained by assertion. If those assumptions are immutable, the product scope must change rather than merely the numeric caps.

RSO7, RSO8, and RSO9 remain rejected. RSO9 is used below only as a costing schema; nothing here revives it or makes an old candidate pass retrospectively. DEC-018 explicitly makes these values a subsequent owner-approval decision: `delivery/decisions/DEC-018.md:16-34`.

The DEC-018 analysis pass edited no implementation files and ran no implementation, product tests, campaigns, installer, deployment, or release action. This document records the proposal for independent review.

## 1. Evidence classes and qualification workload

Three classes must remain separate:

1. **Unavoidable/source-derived lower bounds:** original signatures and objects, reducer key coverage, four copies, 10,000 legal rows, and issuer-wide deletion.
2. **Encoding/schedule models:** current binary command encoding, RSO5 frame/archive overhead, compact indexes, current copy-work multipliers, and a 50× extension of the finite two-transition schedule.
3. **Recommended allowances:** explicit headroom over those models. Headroom is an engineering proposal, not measured uncertainty or a lifetime bound.

The proposed qualification workload includes:

- 10,000 accept-generated rows, including four-vote and five-vote certificates;
- hot-key concentration of all 10,000 references on one issuer/spend;
- one legal attribution/retirement affecting 10,000 rows;
- current and pending state, prover indexes, roots, locks, and failed extents;
- cold, returning, NEW_VIEW, and complete local-`C` recovery;
- 100 transitions and 100 retained configurations, thereby crossing configuration 64;
- exact retries, changed-byte conflicts, orphan/failed evidence, observer/policy work, and prospective funding;
- no per-member, per-view, per-replica, or per-transition cumulative reset.

The original combined semantic limit and issuer-wide behavior are established in `packages/relay/src/witness-handoff-state.ts:40-65` and `packages/relay/src/admission-history-records.ts:261-282`.

## 2. Original-object baseline

### 2.1 Unavoidable certificate floor

RSO9's independently reviewed floor remains valid:

\[
4\ votes \times 256\ B=1,024\ B/accepted\ row
\]

before the request, action, JSON, framing, or encryption. Thus:

\[
10,000\times1,024=\boxed{10,240,000\ B/copy}
\]

and four raw copies require **40,960,000 B**. See `docs/evals/v04-dec017-rso9-rejection-review-2026-11-20.md:9-24`.

### 2.2 More precise current-encoding model

For a canonical four-vote `search` accept, mechanical reconstruction of the existing fixed JSON fields gives:

| Object | Arithmetic | Bytes |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| One vote | fixed fields, values, separators | 555 |
| Four-vote certificate | wrapper + `4*555` + three separators | 2,247 |
| Coordinator request | fixed fields, values, separators | 562 |
| Current binary accept command | `17+562+2,247` | **2,826** |

This follows the request/vote/certificate schemas in `packages/core/src/admission-witness.ts:71-102` and the binary command wrapper in `packages/relay/src/sustainable-recovery-codec.ts:12-18`. It is deterministic encoding arithmetic, not a measured production average. The source report's 553/2,239/560/2,816 B intermediate values were inconsistent with those schemas; the corrected values and all dependent equations are used here without changing the proposed allowances.

With the existing 265 B frame and 256 B archive overhead:

\[
b_{accept}=2,826+265+256=\boxed{3,347\ B/copy}
\]

\[
E_{accept}=10,000\times3,347=\boxed{33,470,000\ B/copy}
\]

A five-vote accept is 3,382 B before framing, or 3,903 B with framing/archive:

\[
E_{accept,5}=39,030,000\ B/copy
\]

Each additional distinct four-vote accept therefore adds **3,347 B/copy** and **13,388 B across four copies**. Deleted semantic rows do not refund their original evidence.

- standalone activation: conservatively bounded by the enclosing 524,288 B policy maximum.

G_{100}=100(524,288+528,384+524,288)+524,288
=\boxed{158,220,288\ B}

including the maximum-size genesis policy. This is a maximum-encoding model, not a claim that ordinary policies have this size.

### 4.1 `C`: evidence, configuration, and delegations

The model is:

\[
\begin{aligned}
C_{model}={}&33,470,000 &&\text{10,000 four-vote accepts}\\
&+158,220,288 &&\text{genesis + 100 maximum configuration transitions}\\
&+384,513 &&\text{201 delegations}\\
={}&\boxed{192,074,801\ B/copy}
\end{aligned}
\]

Immutable evidence is shared by current and pending roots; it is not duplicated merely because two roots reference it. Four copies are nevertheless mandatory:

\[
4C_{model}=\boxed{768,299,204\ B}
\]

Proposed `C=256 MiB` leaves:

\[
268,435,456-192,074,801=\boxed{76,360,655\ B}
\]

That is 39.76% of the modeled obligation. It is explicitly available for protocol originals, changed-byte conflicts, and orphan/failed evidence, but the number and size of those objects are not yet bounded. For scale, it accommodates 22,814 additional minimum four-vote framed accepts; this is arithmetic, not a recommended failure quota.

Five-vote sensitivity:

\[
C_{model,5}=197,634,801\ B
\]

leaving 70,800,655 B under the same proposed allowance.

### 4.2 `D`: prover indexes and current+pending state

The source-derived minimum remains:

\[
D_{dual}=10,000\cdot32+10,000\cdot32
=\boxed{640,000\ B}
\]

before internal nodes or control state. Hot-key current+pending reference lists independently have the same 640,000 B lower-bound scale.

For a complete costing model rather than pretending the minimum is sufficient, assume:

- all 16 authenticated maps can contain 10,000 entries;
- at most `2N` serialized nodes per map;
- 512 B value, 32 B key, 64 B child links, and 8 B metadata: **616 B/node**;
- a full current and pending reservation, despite possible copy-on-write sharing.

Then:

\[
D_{maps}=2\cdot16\cdot(2\cdot10,000)\cdot616
=\boxed{394,240,000\ B}
\]

Add RSO9's 99,628 B finite control subtotal and 100 compact ancestry descriptors at 1,902 B each:

\[
D_{model}=394,240,000+99,628+190,200
=\boxed{394,529,828\ B}
\]

Proposed `D=512 MiB` leaves **142,341,084 B**, or 36.08% of the model. A future candidate using full-depth, non-shared sparse paths rather than an `O(N)` compact representation must measure and preflight the larger actual store; it cannot claim this model by analogy.

Sensitivity per additional fully indexed row under this deliberately conservative model:

\[
2_{\ generations}\times2_{\ nodes/map}\times16_{\ maps}\times616
=\boxed{39,424\ B/row}
\]

The minimum dual-index-only sensitivity is 128 B/current+pending row.

### 4.6 Aggregate entries and metadata

The old observed baseline has 346 entries. The fixed 98-scope recovery layout gives 444. The historical allocation model has at least 28 new identities per returning restoration; 250 analogous returns imply:

\[
E_{100}\ge444+250\cdot28=\boxed{7,444}
\]

before terminal/output and additional failed-path growth. This extrapolation comes from the retained resource audit, which explicitly says it is not a qualified campaign forecast: `docs/developers/v0.4-recovery-resource-budget.md:198-221`.

An 8,192-entry allowance would leave only 748 entries for every unknown terminal/output/failure identity. **Proposed entries=16,384**, leaving 8,940. This larger headroom is justified by the unknown per-cycle terminal/output slope; it is not evidence that 16,384 is a lifetime bound.

This assumes the existing recovery manifest's at-most-1,024-B serialized reservation descriptor. Proposed **metadata=32 MiB**, leaving 15,664,816 B. A future format allowing multi-kilobyte paths per entry must recompute the total rather than reuse this value.

## 5. Work contract

For one 2,826 B accept original, the retained import primitive is:

\[
14b+12A+6H=14(2,826)+12(265)+6(256)
=\boxed{44,280\ B}
\]

Thus:

\[
FrameImport_{10k}=442,800,000\ B
\]
\[
Avail_{10k}=10,000\cdot1,536=15,360,000\ B
\]

Full maximum-configuration ancestry materialization under the existing `15*x` protocol model is:

\[
15G_{100}=15\cdot158,220,288
=\boxed{2,373,304,320\ B}
\]

A complete cold model, including delegation restoration, four ordinary suffix entries, stable/root transfer, is:

\[
\begin{aligned}
Cold={}&442,800,000+15,360,000+79,737\\
&+2,373,304,320+69,936+7,218+8,736\\

**Proposed per-operation `W=4 GiB = 4,294,967,296 B`**, leaving **1,463,337,349 B**, or 51.68% of the cold model.

This is not a CPU-time or memory allowance. Work bytes, RSS, elapsed time, network bytes, and energy remain different quantities.

### 5.2 Ordinary and issuer-wide commands

\[

\[

\[

## 6. Network and deadline sensitivity

### 6.1 Ordinary completion

Using the 2,826 B accept and RSO9's 140,384 B ordinary witness:

```text
five command/witness/T/M deliveries     5*144,786 = 723,930
four original evidence copies            4*2,826 =  11,304
votes, QCs and stable heads                           15,140
                                                     -------
one-attempt application model                       750,374 B
```

One attempt leaves only 298,202 B under the old 1 MiB target before TLS, discovery, retransmission, or failures. Two modeled attempts require 1,500,748 B.

**Proposed ordinary network allowance: 2 MiB/completion**, covering two application-model attempts with 596,404 B remaining. It does not cover unbounded retries. Sensitivity is 750,374 B per additional modeled attempt before transport overhead.

### 6.2 Issuer-wide and recovery completion

At four maximum ordinary proofs per affected row:

\[
|\mathcal W_{issuer}|\le10,000\cdot4\cdot8,774
=350,960,000\ B/voter
\]

\[
Network_{issuer}\approx\boxed{1,755,404,964\ B}

Two attempts require 3,510,809,928 B before TLS/retransmission.

For disjoint recovery, one framed evidence payload is 4,124 B after base64. Evidence plus maximum configuration ancestry gives approximately 1,024,161,536 B including retained availability-reference handling; two such attempts exceed 2 GiB.

**Proposed exceptional maintenance/recovery network allowance: 4 GiB/completion**, leaving 784,157,368 B over the two-attempt issuer-wide model. Ordinary user traffic and exceptional maintenance must be reported separately and also included in lifecycle totals.

### 6.3 Deadlines

Retain the existing deadlines as gates rather than hiding cost by relaxing them:

- **3 s protected ordinary decision;**
- **10 s private operation;**
- **30 s offline evaluation;**
- **5-minute K2 recovery after an authenticated usable quorum exists.**

Required bandwidth sensitivity:

| Envelope | 3 s | 30 s | 5 min |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 2 MiB ordinary | 5.59 Mb/s | — | — |
| 4 GiB exceptional | 11.45 Gb/s | 1.145 Gb/s | 114.5 Mb/s |

These are payload-only rates, before protocol overhead, disk, hashing, signatures, queueing, or retransmission. There is no evidence that volunteers provide them. The 30 s exceptional gate is particularly questionable; it must be demonstrated, not presumed. K2's five-minute gate still requires approximately 114.5 Mb/s at the full four-GiB envelope.

## 7. Memory, power, labor, and availability implications

### Memory

The current 512 MiB model-free relay target is not supported for this envelope. A simple additive working-set screen—not an RSS measurement—is:

```text
largest measured small-fixture process-tree RSS   480,313,344
issuer-wide witness                              350,960,000
maximum configuration objects                   158,220,288
10,000 framed accept contents                     33,470,000
modeled in-memory prover state                   394,529,828
                                                 ---------------
additive screen                                1,417,493,460 B
```

A **2 GiB relay-memory review allowance** would leave 729,990,188 B over that screen. It is not a measured RSS target and must be replaced or validated by actual sustained/startup process-tree measurements. The existing application 1.5/2 GiB targets cannot be retained for a co-hosted prover without an integrated measurement.

### Power and bandwidth cost

No defensible watt or monetary value follows from work bytes. DEC-013's physical evidence is partial, host-confounded, and lacks matched incremental energy: `docs/evals/v04-dec013-physical-evidence-review-2026-11-09.md:30-50`.

Directionally, multi-gigabyte recovery traffic, large indexes, and repeated verification increase bandwidth, I/O, CPU, memory pressure, and energy. Actual incremental power, thermal throttling, storage wear, and network prices remain unmeasured.

### Labor and volunteer purpose

The proposal moves recovery away from a lightweight, occasional volunteer relay toward a provisioned infrastructure role with:

- hundreds of MiB to GiB-scale working state;
- high-bandwidth maintenance/recovery;
- larger durable reserves and repair tails;
- more demanding monitoring and incident response.

Recruitment, enrollment, recovery supervision, and failed-transfer labor may materially increase. No labor hours or economic affordability are inferred.

### Availability

The five/quorum-four/one-Byzantine trust model does not change. Larger transfers and longer recovery increase the exposure window, but no availability percentage follows without a measured schedule.

The existing exact model already requires extremely high overlap: with one permanent withholder, a single cohort needs approximately 99.749% individual availability for a 99% operation target, and two independent cohorts need 99.874%, before common outages: `docs/evals/service-availability-feasibility-2026-10-18.md:48-67`. Larger resource requirements do not improve that arithmetic and may worsen recruitment and correlation.

**Therefore, if the product's purpose requires low-bandwidth, low-memory, casually available personal volunteers, this rebaseline is incompatible with that purpose.** Preserving exact evidence and semantics then requires a product/deployment decision, not just larger numeric limits.

## 8. Qualification under a separately approved contract

All **R1/R2 and K1–K8 remain open**. RSO5's bounded research acceptance, DEC-012's revised research budget, and all rejected RSO7/RSO8/RSO9 designs remain historical evidence only. No old failure becomes a pass under these proposed values.

A future candidate would require:

1. **Owner approval of the actual contract.** Approve exact per-role partitions, ordinary versus exceptional network envelopes, deadline scope, cumulative qualification period, memory assumptions, and operational/economic inputs. DEC-018 itself changes no cap.
2. **Independent architecture review.** Prove exact reducer equivalence, role privacy, four-copy durability, current/pending crash selection, old-cohort fencing, original-genesis continuity, and complete prospective funding.
3. **Complete physical preflight.** Freeze every original object and copy, current/pending index, pending intent, configuration object, metadata descriptor, failed extent, and output identity. Demonstrate 10,000-row, hot-key, issuer-wide, and 100-configuration fit before effects.
4. **Separate implementation authorization and exact-candidate review.** No rejected candidate is revived implicitly.
5. **Executable semantics and faults.** Differential fresh/bound/denied/conflict/split/retry/legacy/retirement tests; at least 1,000 fault scenarios; process death at every durable barrier; zero lost acknowledgments, conflicting accepted spends, resurrected withdrawals, or duplicate notifications.
6. **Lifecycle execution.** Complete 100 transitions, cross 64 configurations, exercise cold/returning/local-loss/NEW_VIEW and same-key replacement, and charge all work to one cumulative ledger.
7. **Workload and resource qualification.** At least 1,000 submitted operations per local/impaired profile across three executions; actual offered load, retries, failures, p50/p95/p99 and confidence intervals; full-edge network, disk/fsync, process-tree RSS, and controlled physical power.
8. **Operational and economic evidence.** A 14-day, at-least-five-operator/three-network pilot, actual recovery arrival rates, labor/repair tails, correlated outages, owner prices/budget/value, and independent affordability review.
9. **No lifetime claim from the fixture.** Any additional unique evidence, configuration, retry, or recovery arrival requires re-preflight and sensitivity analysis against the approved contract.

## Formal verdict

**Recommended for independent review:** `C=256 MiB`, `L=2 MiB`, `P=32 MiB`, `D=512 MiB`, `F=32 MiB`, `W=4 GiB`, cumulative qualification work `8 TiB`, aggregate `8 GiB`, `16,384` entries, metadata `32 MiB`, ordinary network `2 MiB/completion`, exceptional maintenance/recovery network `4 GiB/completion`, and unchanged 3 s/30 s/5-minute deadline gates. A 2 GiB relay-memory allowance is a measurement hypothesis, not an accepted value.

**Not recommended for production approval yet.** Actual object schedules, physical networking, memory, power, labor, availability, and economics remain unresolved. Under unchanged lightweight-volunteer assumptions, no responsible rebaseline is possible without an explicit product/deployment-scope decision. Independent review and a separate owner production decision are both required before any value becomes an approved requirement.
