# v0.4 completion work

The active objective is to complete every v0.4 roadmap item and its necessary
dependencies. The existing branch is `feature/v0.4-private-transport`, based on
the corrected v0.3 work. Implementation, tests, documentation and the draft PR
advance together. This work does not authorize a merge, installer or release.

## Full-scope completion gates

The product owner has approved the [quantified acceptance contract and stop
policy](v0.4-acceptance-kpis.md). Use its persistent blocker IDs and declared
experiments before adding another implementation alternative. Initial priority
is the viability review for fingerprint privacy, collusion/quiet traffic and
composed volunteer availability; independent safety fixes continue in parallel.

On October 18 the product owner withdrew the proposed scope reduction to a
smaller experimental beta. The complete v0.4 roadmap remains the target.
Manual invitations, fixed authorities or disabled epoch rotation are useful
intermediate states, not substitutes for the corresponding completion gates.
The network continues to rely on volunteer devices; this decision does not add
an always-on operator service or a cloud availability dependency.

The gates below organize the existing requirements. They do not add a second
feature roadmap or turn implementation checkpoints into release claims.

| Gate | Required result | Current open boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Private application operation | Real publish, search and mailbox workflows retain their identity separation, durability and deduplication through concurrency, restart and refusal; the calibrated delay scenario passes within the original budgets | Latest delayed run passes five of six paired searches; cold/warm repeatability and broader load remain required |
| Sustainable recovery | Policy, spend and witness histories retain accepted work and permanent denials through safe compaction, owner loss, bounded archive migration and same-key witness replacement | Semantic policy compaction, archive migration and complete replacement/recovery remain open |
| Authority and policy lifecycle | Authenticated authority/member rotation, conflicting-policy handling and emergency distribution preserve existing tokens, evidence and explicit trust assumptions | Rotation/governance and cross-device recovery/distribution are incomplete |

| Fingerprint usefulness and privacy | Independent real-text quality evaluation, cross-community/time privacy targets and epoch-boundary migration pass with the actual embedding path | The measured cross-scope linking and absolute-quality screens fail; live rotation remains disabled |

| Independent operational validation | Independently operated volunteers, Internet/TLS/NAT paths, churn and power conditions satisfy the declared operating envelope; the exact candidate passes local application and cross-platform CI checks | Single-host processes and delay simulation cannot establish independent operation, real availability or energy |

Each gate needs a named scenario, a predeclared acceptance threshold and retained
evidence. Where the existing roadmap has not yet fixed a quantitative target,
fix it before evaluating a candidate solution; do not weaken it after seeing a
failure. Resource and availability claims must identify supported load, devices,
online-quorum assumptions and refusal behavior. No volunteer-only design can
serve while every usable replica is offline; loss of availability must not be
reported as successful delivery or cause accepted history to be discarded.

The concurrent application gate remains open. Before further latency tuning,
complete the bounded viability review above and account for prior alternatives.
Then finish semantic history retention/recovery and authority lifecycle, then complete
automated admission. Fingerprint and observer-privacy design/evaluation can
proceed independently of those changes. Transport/resource audits apply to each
path as it changes, with a final composed check before closure. Prepare the
independent pilot and measurement tooling while local work continues; missing
external observations remain an explicit final gate, not a reason to skip local
implementation or to declare v0.4 complete.

Derived tasks must name the gate they unblock. Broader requests are recorded
separately unless required to meet an existing gate. Closure requires all gates,
not a count of commits, passing unit tests or documented limitations.

## External validation requirements

The roadmap also requires independently operated volunteer overlap, real
Internet/TLS paths, device power/energy and independent-network reachability
measurements. The available single computer can run real local processes,
cryptography, TLS and fault injection, plus explicitly labeled network simulation.
Those results cannot certify independent operators or real volunteer schedules.
Build reproducible pilot tooling and finish all independent implementation work
before treating missing external access or observations as a blocker.

Keep new admission/recovery behavior opt-in until its durability, membership and
freshness gates are actually satisfied. Do not clear old ledgers, reset histories,
discard denial markers or waive a privacy target merely to mark a checkbox done.
