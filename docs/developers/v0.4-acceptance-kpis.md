# v0.4 acceptance KPIs and decision limits

Status: approved by the product owner on October 18, 2026. This is the acceptance
contract for the complete v0.4 scope, following the owner's explicit rejection
of a reduced beta. These thresholds are targets, not claims about the current
implementation. No merge, installer or release follows from recording them.

## Reference experiment

- Reference hardware: four CPU cores, 8 GiB RAM, SSD; record exact CPU, OS, Node,
  model, configuration and commit. Qualify macOS ARM64 and Windows/Linux x64
  separately. A different machine is useful evidence, not reference qualification.
- Retain 10,000 active publications. Nominal load is two simultaneous operations
  per coordinator; eight-request bursts are a separate overload/fairness screen.
  Use closed-loop nominal clients and record achieved throughput, queue time and
  refused attempts so slower work cannot silently change the offered workload.
- Qualify local and impaired-network profiles separately. The impaired profile
  has 50 ms base round trip, independent uniform delay variation of +/-5 ms per
  direction (up to +/-10 ms round trip) and independent 1% packet loss in each
  direction. Freeze seeds before a run. Apply impairment below TCP so handshakes,
  retransmission and connection setup are included. The existing 25 ms byte-stream
  FIFO is an earlier diagnostic profile, not this packet-loss qualification.
- At least 1,000 submitted operations per profile across three independent
  executions; balance publish/search/mailbox and report each separately. Freeze
  trial size and seeds before measurement. Include cold connections, reconnects
  and warm connections. Record cold model download/initialization separately;
  do not discard it from resource reporting or hide it in a warm-operation result.
- A good operation has the correct, durably acknowledged result within its
  original deadline. Unexpected rejection, timeout and uncertain outcome are
  failures, even if a later exact retry succeeds. Charge retry traffic and report
  retries separately. Missing completion is infinite latency, not a fast refusal.
  User-requested local holds are not delivered operations.
- Show counts, denominators, p50/p95/p99, run-to-run variation and 95% confidence
  intervals. Account for clustering by run/device/day where applicable; thousands
  of requests on one host do not provide thousands of independent deployment
  samples. A threshold-straddling interval is inconclusive, not a pass. A larger
  predeclared experiment requires a new recorded decision, not repeated sampling
  until a favorable result appears.
- Store source/data/configuration hashes, command, failures and limitations.
  Measurement must run without concurrent builds/tests on the measured host.

### Measurement definitions and mandatory safety

K2's fault manifest must cover process death during persistence, lost replies,
partitions, stale restores, corrupt input, exhausted reservations and competing
writers. A thousand repetitions of one happy path do not satisfy it. The declared
protocol fault model remains five pinned members, four confirmations, at most one
Byzantine member. Loss of quorum may prevent progress; it must never turn refusal
into acceptance or discard history. RTO starts only when required keys, retained
state and a usable authenticated quorum exist; report earlier outage separately.

K3's 60 seconds measures distribution, not permission to serve stale policy for
60 seconds. Signed cutoff/freshness rules remain authoritative. A disconnected
node cannot receive an emergency update but cannot use that fact to bypass expiry.

K4 denominators are distinct: automation among eligible requests, erroneous
exclusion among eligible requests, erroneous grants among ineligible requests.
Measure fairness over a fixed issuance period, with equal demand and entitlement;
report each group rather than only the mean. Declare the source of eligibility,
attacker control and independent-claim acquisition cost. Resisting 100 identities
for one claim does not prove that one person cannot obtain independent claims.
Issuance/redemption linkability is evaluated under K6 as well as protocol review.

K5 requires held-out relevance labels, not generated labels derived from the
same embedding being evaluated. Keep development and test topics/paraphrases
separate, freeze thresholds before testing, and report each language. Unjudged
cross-pairs are not negative examples. Precision is measured on the notifications
users would receive; candidate-filter quality must also be reported. Report
known-text and reconstruction attacks separately; averaging them is not allowed.

K6 must declare adversary views, compromised roles, auxiliary knowledge,
candidate population, opportunity to issue chosen queries, and observation
window before evaluation. Freeze attack training/test splits by session or user;
do not supply trial boundaries unavailable to a real observer. Include passive
single-hop, cooperating hops, multiple-domain Sybil and cross-session timing
observers. AUC 0.5 is chance and 1 is perfect discrimination. Reversing a poor
classifier's score is allowed; a value below 0.5 is not a privacy success.
Passing finite attacks is not a proof against every possible observer.

K7 measures the complete relevant process tree. Record peak RSS across startup,
unlock and steady operation; report sustained memory over at least 30 minutes at
fixed cardinality. Define idle as no new user work, with enrolled roles and their
ordinary maintenance active, and measure it for 30 minutes. Measure incremental
power on the named physical device against the same OS/brightness/power baseline;
CPU seconds are not watts. Count each physical network edge once in each direction,
including TLS, discovery, issuance, background maintenance and failed attempts.
Report initialization/recovery traffic separately and also in full-lifecycle totals.
Zero completions makes bytes/completion infinite. Disk limits include temporary
replacement peaks and previously funded durability obligations.

Every production inbound/outbound path needs mapped authentication, canonical
signature, bounded-schema/frame/deadline and key-storage enforcement plus negative
tests. A reproducible safety violation blocks adoption regardless of averages.
Successful test counts do not replace review of invariants or external validation.

K8 observes the complete dependency graph. Multiple histories on the same five
devices are not independent cohorts. Every required owner, witness, policy/history
quorum and route must be represented. Measure all wall-clock probe windows, including
unreachable nodes and absent quorums. A passing 14-day pilot qualifies those observed
conditions, not an unrestricted population availability guarantee.

## Stop and decision policy

A blocker owns one persistent identifier across PRs, agents and renamed subtasks.
Before an experiment record its hypothesis, affected KPI, falsifying result,
minimum meaningful improvement, resources and evidence destination.

Stop the affected candidate/line and request a concrete owner decision on the
first applicable condition:

1. A reproducible safety failure or structural counterexample invalidates the
   candidate. Preserve a regression; do not keep tuning that candidate as sound.
2. Two successive substantive alternatives show no improvement distinguishable
   from the predeclared measurement noise or fail the stated meaningful effect.
3. Three substantive alternatives have been evaluated, or five active workdays
   have been spent on this blocker, whichever occurs first. One workday is eight
   accumulated agent/person effort hours; include delegated effort, exclude idle
   waiting. Record intervals prospectively and do not invent historical hours.
4. Progress needs unavailable external measurements or a changed trust/resource
   assumption. Finish independent local work, then mark the specific evidence gap.

A formatting fix, repeated seed or new PR does not reset the counter or count as
a new substantive alternative. All phases of an alternative share its budget.
Historical work is retained; an unknown old count is not reported as zero.

The decision record contains target/baseline/current result, alternatives and
evidence, cause of failure, best remaining option and its bounded cost. Choices
are a newly budgeted continuation, redesign, suspension for a named dependency,
or stopping that approach. Only the owner can approve changed targets/trust/cost
assumptions. Stopping an approach does not complete or defer its requirement.
Other independent authorized work may continue; this policy is not an automatic
request to pause the entire project.

Close a gate after the predeclared checks pass on the exact candidate, with
independent review, required deployment evidence and no safety regression. Stop
optimizing a closed gate unless new evidence or a changed implementation reopens it.
