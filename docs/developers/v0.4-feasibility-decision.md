# v0.4: first decision at the agreed stopping boundary

Status: October 18, 2026. Evidence review complete; the new design budget below
is **proposed, not approved**. All eight [acceptance objectives](v0.4-acceptance-kpis.md)
remain required. Stopping a failed approach does not complete its objective.
There is no merge, release, new cryptographic protocol or background-traffic
enablement in this checkpoint.

## Why a decision is needed now

Three existing assumptions cannot support the corresponding targets by tuning
alone. Public deterministic fingerprints permit known-text enumeration; the
present two hops are linkable when both operators cooperate; and multiple
required volunteer quorums do not inherit the uptime of their individual
devices. These are failures of specified approaches, not a proof that the entire
product vision is impossible.

The agreed stop rule applies to the structural privacy counterexamples. The
concurrency line has also already tried at least four substantive alternatives
(batching, atomic persistence, fresh preparation and connection reuse). Its
counter cannot restart with a new PR or a larger reader batch. Independent
ownership/safety fixes and accurate measurement tooling remain authorized.

## 1. Fingerprint privacy and usefulness — B-FINGERPRINT-PRIVACY

The [actual-model screen](../evals/scoped-fingerprint-real-2026-10-18.md) found
86–97% cross-scope reconstruction and 100% known-text identification among 100
candidates, against the accepted ceiling of 5% for each attack. The static
filter accepted 48/50 labeled negatives. Its relative rotation-quality pass
does not satisfy the new absolute quality requirements, and this small English
fixture does not qualify the bilingual reference corpus.

| Candidate family | What changes | Finding / admission to another experiment |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Public projections, public salts or faster epoch changes | Public representation parameters | Reject as the privacy solution: an observer can compute candidate representations too. Retain as negative controls; no more parameter tuning for this claim. |
| Secret scoped representation / controlled oblivious evaluation | Key access and the ability to evaluate guessed inputs | Research candidate only. A member with the relevant keys, or enough permitted chosen queries, may still enumerate. Ordinary pseudorandom tags do not preserve semantic distance. Define access, collusion, issuance, key loss and query limits first. |
| Confidential similarity computation and retrieval | Where similarity and result selection are computed | Research candidate only. Must protect publications as well as queries, handle malicious participants, bound access-pattern leakage and fit volunteer resources. No existing paper's speed or security result transfers automatically. |

[RFC 9497](https://datatracker.ietf.org/doc/html/rfc9497) describes two-party
oblivious pseudorandom evaluation and its verifiable variants. It is not a
threshold semantic-search protocol. [SANNS](https://www.usenix.org/conference/usenixsecurity20/presentation/chen-hao)
provides a concrete secure nearest-neighbor research reference, with query/result
confidentiality in a semi-honest model. Its stated model is insufficient by
itself for malicious volunteer relays or our publication-privacy requirement.
The judgments in the table are our application-specific design analysis.

## 2. Sparse activity and colluding relays — B-QUIET-COLLUSION

The [cooperating-hop experiment](../evals/private-adversaries-2026-10-18.md)
links 32/32 requests using real signed/encrypted frames in one process. Removing
a shared identifier does not stop a cooperating forwarder from mapping its
incoming and outgoing messages. Separate addresses and keys do not establish
independent operators.

The [current quiet-period policy](v0.4-quiet-period-decision.md) adds zero idle
privacy bytes and wakeups. Consider a permitted sparse situation with no other
client traffic: an observer seeing a client's protocol link can distinguish a
transmission from silence. This is a structural counterexample for activity
hiding in that view, not a measured Internet AUC or a claim about all possible
architectures. Existing unrelated maintenance may obscure some traces, but
there is no invariant that makes its timing hide every isolated operation.

| Candidate family | Limit under the declared strong observers | Decision |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Existing two hops, jitter and short opportunistic queues | Both cooperating hops retain the mapping; sparse traffic may have no honest alternatives | Retain existing limited protections, reject as completion of K6. |
| Additional independent roles / mixing / secret sharing | Requires a precise honest-role assumption and still needs a timing analysis | Consider only with explicit collusion limits; do not replace independent operators with a count of domains. |
| Scheduled client traffic or a different communication design | Could mask idle/active transitions, but has bandwidth, latency, power and synchronization costs | Cost and security study only; current policy does not authorize enabling it. Relay-only noise is insufficient for a client-link observer. |

The [Anonymity Trilemma research](https://freedom.cs.purdue.edu/projects/trilemma.html)
derives bandwidth/latency/anonymity constraints under specified traffic,
coordination and adversary models. It supports examining these costs jointly;
it does not by itself determine an AUC or feasible operating rate for Resonance.

For scale, the [existing arithmetic](../evals/quiet-period-budgets-2026-09-29.md)
uses a 26,708-byte client request/reply exchange: one every ten seconds costs
230.757 decimal MB per connected day and adds up to a 9.5-second p95 slot wait
before network work, under uniform arrivals without backlog. One per minute
costs 38.460 MB/day with a 57-second p95 slot wait. These are examples, not
selected schedules or necessary lower bounds; they exclude TLS, other links and
energy. Neither example qualifies the joint latency and resource targets.

Any positive continuous traffic also makes bytes per useful operation unbounded
as useful traffic approaches zero. The <=1 MiB K7 threshold is for the declared
nominal workload; sparse-mode cost needs an explicit additional envelope. No
silent change to zero-idle policy, the ten-second deadline or an honest-role
assumption is permitted.

## 3. Volunteer availability — B-VOLUNTEER-AVAILABILITY

The new [exact component model](../evals/service-availability-feasibility-2026-10-18.md)
passes 11 tests and independent review. With individual availability of 90%,
one four-of-five cohort has 91.854% availability; two independent required
cohorts have 84.372%. Multiple histories on the same devices share failures and
must not be multiplied as independent cohorts.

With perfect other dependencies and no common outage, two independent cohorts
need individual availability of at least 97.709% to reach 99%. At the K1 floor
of 99.5% conditional operation success, this rises to 98.388%. A 1% common outage
then caps service availability at 98.505%, even with perfect individual uptime.
These are conditional mathematical results, not estimates of our volunteers.

The next viable direction is to map the complete role dependency graph, measure
overlapping volunteer schedules and design safe proactive replacement before
old quorums disappear. Replication placement, trustworthy membership and retained
history transfer must be reviewed together. Lowering confirmation thresholds,
inventing current availability from old receipts or adding operator-run servers
is not an accepted shortcut. The fourteen-day independent pilot remains required.

## Proposed next decision: one bounded architecture pass

**Recommendation:** retain all KPIs and authorize at most **eight aggregate
agent/person hours** for a single joint design pass. This is an additional budget
on the same blocker IDs, not a reset of their history. Maximum three concrete
candidate designs across the pass; no production implementation yet. A candidate
may be rejected early by a counterexample instead of receiving a prototype.

Required deliverables:

1. An explicit adversary and eligibility model, including chosen queries,
   colluding roles, independent-claim acquisition and the minimum honest roles.
2. A complete online dependency/round-trip diagram, privacy argument, durable
   membership transition and failure path for each remaining candidate.
3. A cost table at 10,000 publications for nominal, sparse and recovering modes:
   all-link bytes, latency, memory and what needs physical energy measurement.
4. At most one isolated feasibility prototype for the best candidate if its
   assumptions remain compatible with the agreed targets; no automatic traffic
   or new protocol in the application.
5. A go/no-go record listing the exact incompatible assumptions when no candidate
   fits. Missing evidence is not a pass. Stop at eight accumulated hours, three
   candidates or the earlier safety/structural stopping rule, whichever comes first.

The owner can approve this bounded study, request a specific change to the
target/trust/cost envelope, or suspend these architectural lines. None of these
choices automatically completes v0.4 or authorizes release. The recommended
study avoids committing to another implementation before its premise is tested.
