# R1: certified semantic state and bounded-suffix recovery

Status: October 23, 2026 — model contract; implementation decision pending.
Parent: B-HISTORY-LIFECYCLE / K2 and K3, under the owner-authorized
[replacement roadmap](v0.4-execution-roadmap.md). No runtime adoption.

## Decision being tested

Can a certified semantic state preserve all modeled obligations through two
successive disjoint-cohort replacements, while recovery executes only a bounded
suffix rather than historical business images? A pass supports this representation
under named agreement assumptions. It does not supply that agreement implementation
or close a KPI. A failure must yield a distinguishing retained trace.

## Existing fault and trust boundary

Five pinned members, four confirmations, at most one Byzantine member in each
modeled configuration. Historical honest signatures remain non-equivocating;
the existing handoff explicitly excludes mobile-key-compromise tolerance
(`v0.4-policy-freshness-decision.md`, State and constraint semantics). The same
genesis pins and authenticated configuration chain remain authoritative. A recent
snapshot supplied by a peer is never a fresh trust root.

Agreement is an explicit **specification abstraction**: an immutable,
prefix-consistent order of committed control commands, following the established
PBFT checkpoint/state-transfer structure. Four arbitrary matching signatures or
opaque storage receipts do not implement it. Production consensus selection,
validator access to confidential semantic state and durable network mapping remain
R2 prerequisites. The model must report these assumptions rather than prove them
by assuming a globally honest coordinator.

## State and safety invariants

1. Separate local staging, quorum-protected evidence and external exposure.
   Acknowledgements, emitted votes and retry results require durable recoverability
   before exposure; lost replies retain uncertain charged obligations.
2. Carry original-context partial votes, conflicting proposals, genuine
   certificates, accepted bindings, exact retry outcomes, unexpired rights,
   reservations, permanent denials and withdrawal/fence semantics. Imported
   accepted evidence never becomes destination-local acceptance. Partial conflicts
   refuse; timeout never refunds or establishes that a certificate is absent.
3. A semantic certificate binds original context, configuration, sequence, policy
   cutoff, canonical current state and unresolved evidence. Each honest validator
   checks its complete execution/evidence floor before signing. Required bytes
   remain durably available; a hash is not a replacement for missing data.
4. Reconfiguration uses the same order as mutations/checkpoints. A terminal old
   barrier includes earlier protected work and excludes later old authorization.
   Select one successor durably; conflicting proposals cannot independently win.
5. Four old semantic confirmations plus four new durable installations/readiness
   precede activation. Old/new overlap is not assumed. Preserve the sealed-observer
   barrier and full three-second live authorization drain; restart repeats the
   wait. Offline preparation's 30 seconds never extends a live lease.
6. Checkpoint/recovery share a monotonic configuration/sequence floor. A stale cut
   needs its complete contiguous suffix or refuses. Crash cannot publish partial
   installation or roll back a known floor.
7. Fresh-genesis nodes verify the configuration chain and fresh current authority
   before authorizing. Missing links, payload or quorum refuse. This chain can
   grow even when business replay is bounded.
8. A fixed suffix cap stops new mutations until a certified cut is durable.
   Preserve unchanged physical limits and required originals. Symbolic work counts
   are not bytes, RSS, latency or proof of constant lifetime storage.

## Finite experiment and required distinguishing controls

Use three disjoint configurations, five/four/one each; a fixed small set of
bindings, an uncertain spend, permanent denial, exact retry, policy cutoff,
competing successor proposals and a suffix cap of two commands. Enumerate a
declared finite schedule/state space; report any exploration cap as inconclusive,
never exhaustive. Exercise crash/restart, omitted/missing proof, concurrent
checkpoint/transfer schedules, stale recovery and second-generation inheritance.

Require reachable witness scenarios, not only zero-violation counts over an
empty/unproductive state space. Include a fair four-honest continuation reaching
successor service and an exact retry. Instrument pre-cut business-image visits:
valid recovery must use zero while preserving every modeled obligation.

Unsafe controls must distinguish at least dropping protected uncertain evidence,
accepting an uncertified or incomplete cut, bypassing the old barrier/drain and
using a stale/missing suffix. Preserve trace and rule responsible; do not weaken
the good model to make a negative test pass.

Also retain one separate out-of-scope boundary example: compromising four retired
keys can manufacture an alternative history for a genesis-only client. Label it
excluded by the existing non-mobile fault model, not an in-scope safety finding.
Tolerating that attack would require a new explicitly approved trust mechanism.

## Acceptance and limits

One reviewed model/result with source/config identities, executable command,
finite bounds, mandatory-event coverage, refusal/progress cases, negative traces
and unresolved production mapping. Do not claim timing/resource qualification,
actual crash persistence, real signatures or full Byzantine agreement from this
model. No new runtime dependency, activation, evidence deletion, capacity change,
legacy frozen100 run, CI, build or installer.

## First production mapping to decide after the model

Evaluate semantic cuts signed by four **old logical witness owners**, who already
hold the relevant state and signing keys, bound to the selected epoch proposal,
original context, protected head and complete inherited obligations. These are
different principals from opaque checkpoint-storage replicas. New witnesses
would validate and merge the original-context cuts before existing drain,
installation/readiness and activation rules. Keep original evidence available.

This is a narrower candidate than replacing the complete control plane with a
new global consensus service. The existing epoch protocol must still justify a
terminal order, durable non-equivocation, conflict handling and state completeness.
No choice of a new BFT library is necessary until that mapping is assessed.
This candidate is not approved for runtime activation merely because the model
assumes ordered commands.

The decisive checks are freeze/write serialization; restored-owner freshness
and durable floors; complete partial/uncertain claims; original acceptance and
retry provenance; second-transfer inheritance; and one selected proposal/head
binding through the restart-sensitive three-second drain. A signer cannot create
destination-local acceptance from imported rows. Missing proof refuses. Do not
give ciphertext holders decryption authority or introduce a recent trusted root.

The initial independent review calls this a coherent conditional direction, not
an established production mapping. The model defect has since been corrected;
R1 remains open until the protocol-decision obligations and actual composition
are resolved. A model pass is insufficient.

### Terminal-boundary choice for the mapping

Source assessment identifies an existing mechanism rather than a new consensus
or ad hoc tail protocol: authenticated **history-generation freeze** on four
storage replicas, while each old logical witness still attests semantic state.
Store append checks the durable one-way barrier before accepting even exact
retries; exchange signs only after flushing it. Low-level recovery preparation
collects fresh challenged frozen histories, validates complete compatible data,
selects the longest retained head and persists preparation. Preparation alone
does not allocate or activate a successor.

Required order is: exclude all owner mutations; settle or explicitly retain
pending work; persist logical OLD_FENCED; prepare the existing four-replica
generation freeze; reconcile its exact selected state/head; durably retain the
semantic cut outside the now-read-only generation; then use observer drain and
new installation/readiness/activation. The generic higher-level recovery command
must not run, because it performs additional continuation/provision steps.

This closes storage-generation terminality only. It does not grant semantic
authority to storage replicas, prove an owner's assertions truthful, or prevent
an explicitly authorized later generation. Four individual witness cuts are not
four attestations to identical global state. Original votes/certificates and
policy/retirement proofs must still justify claims, denials and acceptance; raw
owner-asserted strings cannot silently replace full-replay validation or establish
K1/K4 availability/fairness. Semantic-proof completeness remains a decision gate.

The missing production operation is atomic composition of the logical fence,
storage freeze and semantic export under the owner lock, plus its funded durable
receipt and restart path. Freezing shared owner history also blocks unrelated
appends. Returning owners must retain the logical fence and use authenticated
continuation; imported acceptance remains distinct from original-local retry
evidence. These are test requirements, not established production guarantees.
