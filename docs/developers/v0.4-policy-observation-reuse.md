# Bounded infrastructure observation connections

## Problem and decision

Fresh preparation still passed only three of six paired application searches
with calibrated 25 ms one-way stream delay. Each witness repeatedly established
TLS/WebSocket connections to the same pinned observers. Protected admission now
owns reusable infrastructure connections; each call still sends a new challenge
and verifies a fresh observer signature. No policy result becomes cached
authority, and personal-client operation sessions are unchanged.

The gate creates its default pool from its retained five-member observer context.
A supplied transport remains caller-owned; a trusted local transport factory
instead gives the gate a transport and its close method. The two customization
forms cannot be combined. A reopened owner creates a new pool after draining the
old owner. Runtime socket accounting is attached once per physical connection.

## Bounds and failure behavior

- Exact member key and endpoint pins are checked before dialing. Only read-only
  observation frames are accepted by the pool; request data is snapshotted.
- Each member has at most one retained connection and one busy-call overflow:
  ten physical connections at most for the five-member context. Closing sockets
  remain counted until actual closure. A busy call is not stolen or queued behind
  another call; bounded overflow allows the coordinator's own witness to proceed.
- Every connection has its original absolute lifetime, at most ten seconds.
  Reuse requires enough remaining life for the entire new request budget. A call
  does not refresh the connection lifetime.
- Original signed wall time and local monotonic deadlines apply before sending,
  before accepting a reply and after overflow teardown. TLS validation, bounded
  frames, disabled redirects/compression and the handshake limit remain active.
- Replies must authenticate the exact observer and caller challenge. Binary,
  malformed, foreign, replayed, duplicate or unsolicited responses retire the
  connection. Accounting failure prevents the application send.
- Failure, cancellation and successful overflow terminate and drain the socket.
  Shutdown terminates all owned connections and awaits their closure. Review
  found a late-success race while overflow closure was pending; rechecking owner,
  abort and original deadlines after drainage fixes it. Three regressions hold
  actual close delivery while those conditions change.

Listener reuse is explicitly enabled for configured protected observers and the
application fixture. The default listener remains one-use. Reusable listeners
allow one request at a time through reply-send completion, check admission on
every frame, reject pipelining and preserve the original connection deadline.
Their raw connection limit remains eight. Node rejects a ninth connection before
the application can evict an idle one, so completed idle connections are retired
proactively to leave one admission slot. Raw, incomplete or active connections
are not treated as idle. Destroyed connections remain accounted until close.

## Review and evidence

Independent review covered ownership, freshness, overflow, capacity, listener
admission and drain races. Fifteen pool tests, 31 listener tests and 39 existing
freshness/configuration/runtime tests pass: 85 distinct tests in eight files.
The TypeScript build and strict standalone evaluator typecheck pass. Pool and
listener reuse tests are included in the three-platform ownership CI job.

The [actual application measurement](../evals/v04-policy-reuse-2026-10-18.md)
passes five of six paired searches and all eight other checkpoints. Concurrent
connections fall from 282 to 210 and TCP bytes from 5,113,879 to 4,741,389.
The remaining concurrent search still fails its original deadline. This change
reduces setup cost but does not close the latency gate, prove arbitrary-client
listener fairness or establish public Internet operation.
