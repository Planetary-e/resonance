# CometBFT sidecar recovery architecture — DEC-006 design exploration

**Status: REJECTED before implementation; independent review CONFIRMS REJECTION.**
This is a design-only exploration, not implementation authorization, a product test
result, or acceptance of FEAT-001, R1/R2, EVL-003/004/005/015, or any K gate. Three
mandatory operations independently exceed the unchanged per-operation work limit.
The displayed cumulative arithmetic is rejected and is not a protocol-derived or
complete CometBFT forecast. The listed physical reservations are incomplete and do
not establish a full CometBFT storage bound.

No dependency, production source, product test, campaign, Git/CI, installer, release,
threshold, trust model, or fixture was changed. No 100-transition, 3 s/30 s, energy,
or real process-tree measurement is claimed.

## 1. Decision scope and established-method comparison

DEC-006 authorizes one genuinely different, bounded exploration while preserving:

- exact original business objects, signatures, reducers, rights, denials and
  uncertainty;
- five pinned member identities, quorum four, at most one Byzantine participant,
  and four independently durable evidence/application copies;
- role-private issuer, wallet, evidence and delegation state;
- prospective funding before promises and complete crash/recovery accounting;
- C=262,144 B, L/P=65,536 B each, D=524,288 B, F=32,768 B,
  operation W=4,718,592 B, cumulative 256 MiB, aggregate 2 GiB/512 entries/1 MiB
  metadata, and 3 s/30 s deadlines.

The comparison considered crash-only Raft, another custom RSO representation, and
an established BFT replicated state machine. Raft cannot provide the required
Byzantine authentication and is not a substitute. Another custom journal would
repeat the rejected RSO line rather than explore a different agreement substrate.
CometBFT plus the existing Node business application was therefore the only
candidate advanced to a concrete forecast.

The selected shape has **one CometBFT agreement journal**. It has no RSO agreement
or delta journal. A separate append-only encrypted evidence/delegation store is an
availability and private-recovery store, not a second source of agreement.

## 3. Process and data architecture

Each funded member runs a pinned Go CometBFT sidecar and the existing Node business
application, connected over a private local ABCI transport. The five pinned
identities and quorum-four rule remain; the forecast funds four executing durable
copies. A consensus vote is not a durable evidence copy and cannot substitute for
one.

There are three distinct data planes:

| Plane | Authority and contents |
| --- | --- |
| CometBFT agreement | Ordered blocks, votes, commits, validator/configuration changes and the single agreement history. |
| Node application | Deterministic common business state, current/pending committed application snapshots, AppHash, rights/denials/uncertainty and policy/observer state. |
| Private availability | Append-only encrypted original evidence, delegations and recovery metadata, with recipient keys held only by their authorized private owner. |

The application retains the exact original business verifier and reducer as the
semantic authority. Every validator derives byte-identical canonical outcomes and
AppHash from the same ordered inputs. Floating point, wall-clock time, random map
iteration, local filenames, private indexes, network arrival order and local cache
state cannot enter consensus results.

Private processing may authenticate/decrypt original inputs and build incremental
indexes, but any optimization must produce exactly the full original reducer's
bytes, including censorship, conflict, denial, split, retry and uncertainty cases.
A private cache is never alternative evidence and cannot change AppHash.

Encrypted evidence/delegation ciphertext and private recipient keys remain outside
public AppHash and replica plaintext. AppHash may commit to public deterministic
application state and opaque authenticated identities; it must not become a ledger
of private plaintext or a substitute for original-signature verification.

## 4. Funding, commit and crash boundaries

A proposed operation follows this boundary:

1. Authenticate the exact original object and its historical authority.
2. Prospectively reserve all C/L/D/P/F, sidecar, snapshot, network and recovery
   extents before proposal or vote.
3. Append encrypted evidence/delegation records and obtain four independently
   durable matching acknowledgments.
4. Stage the deterministic application transition and its current/pending state.
5. Only then may the proposal/vote path proceed.
6. `FinalizeBlock` verifies already-funded records and computes the deterministic
   result. `Commit` atomically persists the application result before returning.

A pre-consensus copy that never reaches a committed block is an orphan: retained
and charged if necessary, but with no business effect. A crash between evidence
funding, votes, `FinalizeBlock` and `Commit` must select either the complete prior
committed state or the complete committed result, never a partially applied
business transition. Durable evidence availability alone does not authorize state
advancement.

Four votes cannot be counted again as four application/evidence copies. Conversely,
four evidence copies cannot be treated as agreement. Both obligations are included
in the forecast.

Block pruning may remove obsolete public block payloads only after all recovery
obligations remain funded. It must never delete original evidence needed to prove
rights, denials, conflicts, retries or uncertainty. Pruning is not evidence
compaction and cannot be used to make this forecast fit.

## 5. Membership, transfer and private recovery

CometBFT validator changes obey the source's H+2 activation rule. Business issuer,
witness and recipient cutoffs are separate authenticated application transitions.
A validator update cannot silently replace an issuer/witness key at the same
height. Old authority remains valid only through its exact cutoff; new service
requires the activated configuration, fresh policy and currently delegated private
keys.

Cold recovery has two independent halves:

- **Public agreement/application:** state sync offers a trusted AppHash/height and
  snapshot chunks; block sync replays an authenticated suffix when required.
- **Original private availability:** independently verify original signers,
  historical scope/configuration chains, exact evidence digests and delegation
  authorization, then privately unwrap recipient keys and decrypt locally.

A valid AppHash does not authenticate a role-private delegation or evidence frame.
A valid original evidence frame does not prove that it is selected by committed
agreement. Both checks must pass before service. Corrupt chunks, spoofed snapshots,
unknown scopes, stale delegations and digest-race substitutions refuse before
effects.

Returning/new-view transfer sends missing authenticated application state, block
suffixes and private delegations; it does not assume predecessor availability.
After old-set freeze, each successor must hold all selected evidence/delegations
with four durable copies. Complete local private recovery is tested with the
predecessor offline and local private state removed. Replica replies remain
ciphertext and signed metadata, never replica plaintext or recipient private keys.

## 8. Operational and security assessment

The sidecar replaces custom BFT agreement machinery but introduces a second
language/runtime, a Go process supervisor, ABCI framing and lifecycle coupling,
genesis/configuration management, validator-key operations, snapshot/chunk serving,
WAL/block retention and dependency provenance. These are real operational costs,
not free maturity benefits. The Node reducer and private evidence/delegation
problems remain local responsibilities.

A future candidate would need explicit process-tree resource metering covering the
Go sidecar, Node application, children, IPC buffers and shutdown/restart behavior.
It would also need supply-chain review and an authenticated local deployment
threat model. This document supplies none of those implementation results.

No inference about 100 transitions is made from this two-transition exploration.
Likewise, byte-work forecasts do not establish 3 s/30 s latency or energy use.
Real maximum-envelope network, crash and process measurements would remain
necessary even for a design whose work forecast fit.

## 10. Falsifier for any later separately authorized candidate

A future proposal must first show a coherent way to satisfy every mandatory limit
without deleting work or weakening semantics. Only a separate owner decision could
authorize implementation of a falsifier, which would require:

1. **Denial equivalence:** full-original and optimized reducers produce byte-exact
   outcomes for censorship, conflicts, denials, splits, retries and uncertainty.
2. **Crash atomicity:** process death at evidence append, each acknowledgment,
   proposal, vote, `FinalizeBlock`, application write and `Commit` yields exact
   replay, no partial business effect, no lost prepared/committed state and no
   orphan effect.
3. **Funding before commit:** insufficient C/L/D/P/F, sidecar, snapshot or recovery
   space prevents proposal/vote promises; four votes never substitute for four
   durable copies.
4. **Membership freshness:** H/H+1/H+2 schedules cannot activate stale validators,
   issuer/witness keys or delegations, and cannot replace keys at an unauthorized
   same-height cutoff.
5. **Snapshot authentication:** spoofed AppHash/height/chunks, corrupt evidence,
   digest-race substitution and stale delegation all refuse before service;
   block-sync suffix replay preserves the same outcome.
6. **Independent private recovery:** cold, returning, new-view and complete local
   loss recover after predecessor shutdown with original-signature verification,
   four-copy availability and no replica plaintext or exposed recipient key.
7. **Retention:** pruning and failed-tail recovery never remove original evidence
   required for rights, denials, conflicts, retries or uncertainty.
8. **Real resource closure:** instrument the actual Go+Node process tree, ABCI/
   protobuf/network copies, WAL/block store, snapshots and private recovery under
   the unchanged W and cumulative limits; separately measure the maximum-envelope
   3 s/30 s deadlines and energy.

Passing a primitive or a small lifecycle would not itself establish 100-transition
fit or any EVL/K gate. This falsifier is not authorization to implement the
rejected sidecar design.
