# Reserving a successor with complete history

Status: **experimental reservation and write-back, 2026-10-15**. This extends
[frozen recovery preparation](v0.4-history-checkpoint-recovery.md). It copies the
selected encrypted history to four pinned volunteers and collects their durable
commitments to one successor proposal. It does **not** activate that generation,
restore application state, or enable recovery in the live app.

## Implemented ordering

`createHistoryCheckpointSuccessor`, exported by `@resonance/relay`, requires the
same independently retained context, owner key, root, predecessor generation,
requested successor generation, local encryption key and five pinned endpoints
as the recovery coordinator. Explicit initialization additionally requires a
complete `HistoryRecoveryPreparation` with four compatible frozen-head receipts.

1. Validate the entire selected chain and its four frozen anchors. Create one
   owner-signed proposal binding the context, root, both generations, selected
   sequence/digest, four receipts and a random nonce. Persist the complete
   selection and exact proposal in encrypted local state before any network I/O.
2. On explicit `reserve()`, contact all five members using a fresh challenge and
   validity window. Request the same freeze barrier and verify each member's
   signed current head is a prefix of the selected chain. A longer, conflicting
   or differently frozen history cannot count.
3. Send only the missing suffix, one block per authenticated `repair` request.
   Each request binds both the proposal and the block. The replica checks the
   owner signature, sequence, predecessor and storage budget, then flushes the
   block and manifest before replying. Repair preserves the freeze; it cannot
   issue an ordinary append acknowledgement.
4. Send `seal` with the full proposal. Each replica requires the entire exact
   selected chain, including every shorter frozen-head anchor, before recording
   its reservation. An honest replica durably reserves **one proposal per
   predecessor generation**. It rejects competing proposals and higher freeze
   barriers after reserving; an exact retry is allowed.
5. Require four distinct, signed `sealed` replies for the same proposal and
   current attempt. Persist the certificate before returning it. A replica
   signing only a digest without retaining the complete payload does not follow
   this protocol. With at most one dishonest member, four receipts imply at
   least three honest complete copies, not that all four signers can be trusted.

The old generation remains frozen after success. A reservation certificate is
neither a normal write certificate nor permission to start a successor writer.
The successor's initial checkpoint, transition ancestry and activation evidence
are not implemented. These remain separate prerequisites for operational use.

## Concurrency: safety boundary and known blocking case

Two four-member quorums among the same five identities intersect in at least
three members. With at most one Byzantine member, at least two intersection
members are honest. If honest members retain their one-proposal commitment,
two distinct proposals cannot both collect four reservations. This is a narrow
intersection argument for this primitive, under durable-state and fixed-member
assumptions; it is not a proof of a complete recovery protocol. The distinction
between quorum intersection and a full protocol follows
[Malkhi and Reiter's Byzantine quorum work](https://malkhi.com/files/byzquorums-STOC1997.pdf).

**The primitive can permanently block after competing partial reservations.**
For example, two honest members reserve proposal A, two reserve B, and the fifth
member signs both. Each proposal has three receipts; neither can reach four.
All devices being online does not resolve this split. Even proposals selecting
identical data compete if their signed proposal identities differ.

The general failure of irrevocable one-vote acceptors to guarantee progress is
also explained in section 2.2 of
[Lamport's Paxos Made Simple](https://lamport.azurewebsites.net/pubs/paxos-simple.pdf).
That paper uses a crash-failure model; its multi-ballot protocol is not an
implemented or established Byzantine recovery solution here.

Do not erase reservations, increment a generation, replace members, or reduce
the quorum to bypass a split. Safe progress under competing recovery attempts
needs a separately reviewed protocol that preserves already possible
certificates. Until then, reservation remains an isolated experimental API.

## Interrupted attempts and retained evidence

- Reopening sends nothing. A retry uses the persisted proposal, never a newly
  generated nonce, and requires fresh replies even if a certificate is saved.
  Lost replies and ordinary outages can therefore be retried without changing
  commitments. A concurrent competing proposal can still prevent progress.
- Each retry queries the current head and skips already retained blocks. Upload
  resumes at complete block boundaries. The preceding full-chain download still
  must finish within one attempt; resumable download is not implemented.
- A failed copy or reservation never deletes accepted blocks, lowers a barrier,
  or clears a vote. Uncertain filesystem writes poison the handle until reopen.
  Failed coordinator certificate persistence cannot return successful recovery.
- Replica manifests retain the proposal across restart. Pre-reservation
  manifests without the new field can still be opened. Independently retained
  `minimum.successorId` can detect rollback that erases a reservation while
  leaving the same head/barrier. Entire valid-directory rollback remains
  undetectable if all external anchors are lost as well.
- The exact proposal and selection are persisted in `history-successor.json`.
  Missing coordinator state fails closed. Retrieval of a lost proposal from
  volunteers is not yet exposed over the network; do not initialize a new
  proposal as a replacement for a missing file.

## Bounds and deployment

- Proposals are limited to 16 KiB. They contain four compact freeze receipts,
  not the full payload. The dedicated checkpoint frame limit adds that bounded
  metadata to the existing envelope limit and 4 KiB framing allowance. Ordinary
  relay frame limits are unchanged.
- The encrypted coordinator file is limited to 96 MiB, plus its temporary atomic
  replacement. Selected histories retain the 10,001-block / 64 MiB recovery
  bounds. Replica capacities and owner pauses still apply to missing blocks;
  they cannot discard accepted obligations. These are not peak-memory bounds.
- One active attempt per coordinator, five member workers, one block in flight
  per worker and a maximum/default 30-second attempt deadline. Both wall-clock
  expiry and monotonic elapsed time are enforced. Four receipts cancel remaining
  exchanges; late replies cannot complete a cancelled attempt.
- The test listener is loopback-only. Public endpoints require independently
  configured WSS. This work provides no discovery, Internet availability or
  independent-operator guarantee. No continuously operated relay is introduced.
- Payloads remain opaque ciphertext. Actual spend/denial semantics and policy
  freshness still require validation before any reviewed atomic restoration.
  File/directory flush semantics retain the documented Windows limitation.

## Validation

```sh
npm run build
npx vitest run --maxWorkers=1 history-checkpoint history-exchange
npm test -- --maxWorkers=1
```

TypeScript compilation and all **9 focused files / 115 tests** pass locally in
93.90 seconds. New tests cover four complete copies, missing-suffix-only upload,
partitions and lost replies, fresh retry, duplicate/forged/cross-attempt receipts,
incomplete histories, proposal substitution, capacity and pause, failed replica
and coordinator flushes, external rollback anchors, deadlines and cancellation.
Concurrent schedules cover a certified winner and the blocked two/two split
with one equivocating member. Real loopback services exercise volunteer restart
and SIGKILL of pending/certified coordinator processes.

The complete local suite passes **122 files / 706 tests** in 397.10 seconds with
one worker, including 15 new successor tests. Production source was unchanged
throughout that run. Remote CI is recorded separately in draft PR #34. These
controlled tests do not certify arbitrary recovery schedules, power loss or
multi-device Internet behavior.

## Next boundary

Design and verify progress under competing reservations together with
certificate-controlled activation and cross-generation ancestry. Preserve
transition evidence through crashes and repeated recovery. Then finish resumable
download, live lagging-replica repair, mandatory replication before protected
admission outputs, quorum-safe compaction and reviewed atomic restoration. No
reservation result alone reopens a missing or damaged live history.

Follow-up: the [view-change decision and executable model](v0.4-successor-view-change-decision.md)
evaluate a replacement with separate preparation/confirmation and four-report
view changes. Bounded agreement and conditional progress checks pass; the current
one-shot API remains unchanged and still has the split-vote limitation above.
