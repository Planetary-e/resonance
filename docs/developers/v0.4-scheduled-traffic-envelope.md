transport analysis, not to application activation, MPC implementation or a
claim that K1, K6 or K7 passes.

The steady allocation is **56.25 MiB/access-hour and 225 MiB/all-link-hour**.
The ceilings leave 7.75/31 MiB respectively for enrollment and other boundary
costs, not additional average traffic. A continuously enrolled day would allocate
1,350 MiB at the client access edge and 5,400 MiB across all links. These are
experimental ceilings and conditional arithmetic, not approved background-data
defaults, measured consumption, or a promise of affordability on a device.

Count each physical edge once in each direction, including headers, ACKs, TLS,
retries, setup and renewal. Two nominal clients allocate 128 KiB/s jointly;
achieved throughput must exceed 0.125 completions/s merely to leave any headroom
below 1 MiB/completion at that full steady allocation. This necessary arithmetic
condition does not establish a workable closed-loop load or fair shared service.

## Frozen schedule family

Before running results, Astra approved three segmentation sensitivities within
one schedule family. None changes its rate or creates another architecture.

| Slot | Wire-byte allocation per direction/edge | Useful bytes assumed | Overhead reserve |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 500 ms | 4,096 | 3,072 | 1,024 |
| 250 ms | 2,048 | 1,536 | 512 |
| 125 ms | 1,024 | 768 | 256 |

The 25% overhead reserve is an assumption to falsify, not measured TCP behavior.
Smaller cells increase transmission/wakeup frequency; they do not inherit a power
or overhead pass from the larger cell. A fixed write size also does not guarantee
fixed physical packet timings or sizes.

The offline pipeline has four directed FIFO lanes, two edges for the request and
two for the reply. A cell takes a whole slot and may advance only one edge per
slot. The reply starts only after all request fragments arrive and the explicit
processing delay elapses. Loss retries occupy later fixed slots. Queue refusals,
deadline failures and interrupted enrollment remain in the original denominator.
The modeled fixed allocation is charged throughout enrollment, including silence.

Enrollment, renewal, departure and sleep schedules are fixed independently of
the operations. They remain visible to observers. An operation arriving just
before sleep cannot resume in a later session as if the interruption never
happened. Starting a session on demand, adding overflow cells, changing
destinations or extending a session for pending work violates this model.

## Results and falsifiers

The frozen inputs use the historical 15,495-byte request / 11,213-byte reply
example, seven processing delays `[0,250,500,1000,1125,1250,2000]` ms and 100
deterministic arrival phases per profile: **2,100 isolated model runs**. These
sizes are an example, not a universal lower bound or the future MPC encoding.

| Slot | p95 with zero processing | Largest tested processing delay meeting local p95 <=6 s | p95 at that delay |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 500 ms | 6,470 ms | None | — |
| 250 ms | 5,485 ms | 500 ms | 5,985 ms |
| 125 ms | 4,868 ms | 1,125 ms | 5,993 ms |

At 125-ms slots, 1,250 ms processing fails the local screen (p95 6,118 ms).
Do not retune the overhead reserve or the processing input after observing this
boundary. Processing is hypothetical work **after** request delivery, not an
old full-application latency added a second time.

Additional fixed cases cover an idle hour, one sparse operation, one operation
on each of two clients, an eight-request burst with queue capacity two, sleep /
reconnect, two synthetic dropped cells and over-budget startup. Idle and active
hourly allocations match. The fine burst completes two operations and explicitly
rejects six; its p95 remains infinite. Sleep completes only the new post-reconnect
operation; the interrupted and asleep submissions remain unsuccessful. Startup
overruns are reported even when the unconstrained pipeline completes an operation.
Caps are report-only checks here, not an implemented runtime budget controller.

Reject a candidate if payload/overhead/retries exceed an edge or session budget,
latency exceeds its original target, operation activity changes the visible
schedule, nominal cost exceeds 1 MiB/completion, or measured power exceeds K7.
Passing a latency column cannot compensate for any other failure.

## Evidence limits and next gate

The dedicated lanes and fixed processing delay do not model coordinator
contention. The two-client sample is not the required closed-loop nominal run.
The two-edge geometry also retains adjacent cooperating operators: a fixed
schedule alone does not prevent them from mapping forwarded messages. An honest
mixing role or other transport redesign needs its own trust argument and must
charge every additional edge and delay. The reported processing headroom cannot
be assigned entirely to MPC while ignoring those costs. Pre-request embedding,
wallet preparation and startup are also absent from this model and consume part
of the actual application deadline.
The eight-second column in the JSON is a threshold comparison, not the impaired
network profile. Synthetic dropped cells do not model TCP packet loss. The
other-edge reserve charges costs but supplies no implementation, timing schedule,
privacy argument, MPC rounds or secure-computation performance result.

Before any activation, measure physical packets and energy with the exact
payload/transport, include every observer-visible edge, implement budget refusal
without operation-driven overflow, and test full-session adversaries. Equal byte
totals alone cannot establish AUC <=0.55. Collusion/output/eligibility decisions
and a reviewed confidential-computation protocol remain separate prerequisites.
The local/impaired application, independent volunteer and power gates remain open.
