# RSO4 recovery architecture — SECOND REVISION, REJECTED

**Final design-review status: RSO4 REJECTED. No implementation is justified.**
This is a design-only record, not acceptance or authorization. R1/R2 and K1–K8
remain open. No resource, trust, timing or acceptance gate changes are proposed.

## 0. Review history

RSO2/immutable-binary lacked predecessor-independent recovery. RSO3 added that
property but measured bootstrap 9,959,445 B and first reserveInstall 9,335,006 B,
then failed C at 270,204 > 262,144.

The first RSO4 draft used five scopes, full catalog rewrites and unsupported work
allocations. The first REVISE required ten scopes, hot snapshot+delta generations,
immutable frame-identity proof roots, explicit revocation and bottom-up work. The
revision used local-last full-snapshot compaction and computed C=254,099 B and
cumulative work=229,049,410 B.

The second independent REVISE correctly rejects that model: local-last replacement
can leave neither complete generation with four copies; compaction changes delegated
heads without funded delegation; per-scope deltas were undercounted; signed pack
manifests were omitted; returning payload, creation multipliers and operation counts
were wrong. Corrected partial cumulative work was already 261,084,218 B before new
head/manifest/pending-generation terms. No arithmetic in this document should be
read as permission to fit the caps.

## 1. Required sound generation protocol

A sound revision needs two-phase generation installation:

1. **Prepare pending.** Construct and fully authenticate the new stable snapshot,
   delta chain and any required new delegation. Fund and store pending records
   independently of the previous complete generation.
2. **Replicate pending.** Each replica validates the complete pending generation and
   fsyncs it without deleting the previous complete state.
3. **Commit only after four.** Once four matching pending generations are durable,
   install/commit locally. Only then may old snapshots/deltas be retired and the
   journal advance.

This preserves availability in mixed crashes:

| New pending copies | Previous state | New state | Required selection |
| ---: | --- | --- | --- |
| 0 | five complete copies/local floor | absent/incomplete | previous complete generation |
| 1 | five complete copies/local floor | one pending copy | previous complete generation |
| 3 | five complete copies/local floor | three pending copies | previous complete generation |
| 4 | previous state retained until local commit | four complete pending copies | either complete generation; commit locally, then new generation |
| local commit | old records may retire | five copies | new complete generation |

A torn pending record cannot damage the previous state. A pending generation and
its delegation/head records are real physical obligations, not free transients.

This is the only crash-correct form of the proposed full-snapshot compaction. The
former local-last overwrite model is rejected.

## 2. Delegation, proof roots and rotation

A recovery reference remains `scopeId, proofId, kind, frameDigest, frameBytes,
packDigest, offset`. Proof roots cover immutable frame identity, not catalog version.

A snapshot+delta generation satisfies a root only with exact entry inclusion,
authorized original scope/configuration/rotation history and a canonical complete
superset of the selected references. Per-scope entry counts `(e_i)` are authenticated
and `sum(e_i)=72`; this is not 72 entries per scope. `previousDigest` alone is not
consistency evidence.

A same-key delegation may authorize newer snapshots by authenticated superset. It
binds scope key, original configuration, proposal, minimum snapshot version and a
currentness floor. A newer snapshot is accepted only when it is complete, authorized,
four-copy plus locally installed and a superset of both the delegated floor and
selected proof root. Service also requires fresh policy/current keys. A stale bundle
cannot justify reservation or serving.

Scope-key rotation/revocation changes the complete generation and requires a new
funded delegation and pending snapshot. If those bytes do not fit the finite
manifest, rotation refuses before effects; it is not treated as lifetime acceptance.
Revocation remains forward-only: only plaintext already obtained by a removed
recipient is guaranteed. Current recipients need the new complete key/catalog
generation for selected old proofs; stale delegation cannot activate them.

## 3. Records included in this closure

The rejected model retains:

- immutable `RSO4F001` frames, each `plaintext + 169` bytes;
- at most 31 `RSO4P001` packs with one 256-byte archive header each;
- stable `RSO4C001` snapshots, `568 + 64*entries` per scope;
- at most four `RSO4C101` deltas **per scope**, therefore 40 possible deltas across
  ten scopes, each `472 + 64*entries` for one-entry worst-case accounting;
- three `RSO4D001` delegation bundles, `553 + 152*scopeCount` each;
- pending complete snapshots and signed generation heads.

### Newly explicit signed pack manifest and range records

Each pack has a signed manifest:

```
magic[8] || packDigest[32] || frameCount[4] || signature[64]
  || repeated(frameDigest[32] || offset[4] || length[4])
```

Manifest size is `108 + 40*frameCount`. A signed range response is:

```
scopeId[32] || packDigest[32] || offset[4] || length[4] || signature[64]
```

Range size is 136 B excluding the already counted frame payload. The closure prices
all 31 manifests and 72 range records. Range responses are authenticated
availability evidence, not unsigned mutable slices.

## 4. Exact physical rejection

Logical selected inventory remains `P=72`, `B=215344`, distributed across up to ten
scopes. The following includes frame contents, pack headers, every signed manifest
and range, current and pending stable snapshots, the maximum 40 deltas and three
full ten-scope delegation bundles:

```
frame contents = 215344 + 72*169             = 227,512
pack headers   = 31*256                       =   7,936
pack manifests = 31*108 + 72*40               =   6,228
signed ranges  = 72*136                       =   9,792
current stable = 72*64 + 10*568               =  10,288
pending stable = 72*64 + 10*568               =  10,288
40 deltas      = 40*(472+64)                  =  21,440
delegations    = 3*(553 + 152*10)             =   6,219
                                                 ---------
C total                                         299,703
C limit                                         262,144
C excess                                         37,559
```

The pending stable generation alone would push the model over C. Even removing all
40 deltas and every signed range would leave 268,471 B, still 6,327 B over. Those
records cannot be removed without breaking crash availability or authenticated
range recovery.

F remains independent. Preparation must fund complete failed extents for previous
and pending catalogs, packs, deltas, delegations and journals. If existing failed
bytes plus the complete required extent exceed 32,768, the operation refuses before
modifying the previous generation. F cannot be used to hide the C rejection.

Catalogs/delegations remain within C and add no reservation components. Aggregate
2 GiB/512/1 MiB arithmetic does not rescue per-owner C.

## 5. Corrected bottom-up work accounting

The corrected creation and import multipliers are stated explicitly:

- creation: original input/ciphertext/direct check and comparison (`15b`) plus one
  frame allocation, five replica buffers and six writes (`11*(b+169)`), or
  `15b + 11*169` per proof;
- existing-frame import: source/read/decrypt/compare (`14b`) plus twelve frame-body
  buffer/write obligations (`12*169`), or `14b + 12*169` per proof.

Returning new payload is 28 frames/22,000 B plus one 16,384 B policy and one 12,288 B
activation: **50,672 B**, not 46,576 B. For 30 records and 30 packs:

```
returning payload = 14*50672 + 12*169*30 + 6*256*30
                  = 816,328 B
```

Agreement count is six commands × five phases (propose/prepare/commit/execute/
finish) × four members = **120 operations**, not 96. Seven retries are included.

The second-review corrected partial cumulative total is **261,084,218 B** before
the newly explicit terms below.

### Additional required work terms

Using the same named buffer/validation/write model (eleven materializations for a
signed small record, 128 work bytes per encrypted catalog entry):

| Newly priced term | Formula | Work |
| --- | --- | ---: |
| Signed pack manifests | 11*(31*108 + 72*40) | 68,508 |
| Signed range records | 11*(72*136) | 107,712 |
| Increase from 4 total to 40 per-scope deltas | (40-4)*(11*(472+64)+128) | 216,864 |
| Pending full stable generation for 24 compactions | 24*122,384 | 2,937,216 |
| Signed generation heads for 10 scopes × 24 compactions | 24*10*11*404 | 1,066,560 |
| **Additional work** | | **4,396,860** |

A generation head is `magic[8] || scopeId[32] || keyVersion[4] || generation[8] ||
headDigest[32] || signature[64] || archiveHeader[256]`, 404 B including archive.
Same-key superset delegation avoids a new delegation per compaction, but pending
snapshot/head work remains mandatory.

Numerically:

```
261,084,218 + 4,396,860 = 265,481,078 B
268,435,456 - 265,481,078 =   2,954,378 B
```

This arithmetic does not rescue RSO4. It excludes any new lifecycle not covered by
the corrected partial model and supplies no timing evidence. Physical C is already
rejected, and the small work margin cannot be used as acceptance headroom.

## 6. Required tests if a future architecture is proposed

Any future model must distinguish mixed-generation crashes at 0/1/3/4 pending
copies and local commit; exact signed head/catalog-entry/frame availability;
previous-generation survival during torn pending writes; F exhaustion before any
survivor modification; same-key superset versus stale delegation; and rotation that
refuses before effects when its complete generation is unfunded.

It must also test stale/cold catch-up, second-transfer current exporter, recipient
leaving, predecessor-unavailable recovery, and incremental evidence evaluation
against full original reducers for every conflict/denial/split/retry case. No
incremental index may change original business results.

Hard falsifiers remain C>262,144, W>4,718,592, cumulative work>256 MiB, hidden
copies/participant resets, missing complete-generation availability, privacy/safety
failure, or 3 s/30 s failure.

## 7. Verdict and remaining review questions

**RSO4 is REJECTED.** The complete physical model is 299,703 B against C=262,144,
37,559 B over. The crash-correct pending-generation requirement is fundamental, not
an optional overhead. Removing pending state would reintroduce the availability
bug; removing manifests, ranges or deltas would break authentication or the declared
inventory. No catalog-primitive, cold-bootstrap or transfer/compaction implementation
slice is justified.

Remaining questions for any fundamentally different future architecture:

1. Can an append-only entry-log/checkpoint representation prove old and new complete
   state availability without pending full-catalog storage or hidden mutable/torn
   records? It must be proven at 0/1/3/4 copies, not asserted.
2. Can that representation retain exact signed head/entry/frame availability and
   canonical proof-root supersets under every mixed-generation crash?
3. What is the complete declared scope/proof/pack/delta/delegation manifest and its
   F-funded failure matrix, including rotation refusal?
4. Can incremental evidence indexes reproduce full original reducer outcomes without
   full-history decryption in every business case?
5. What independent maximum-envelope work, real-copy/network and 3 s/30 s evidence
   would remain after a physically sound design exists?

The corrected cumulative arithmetic and all physical formulas are preserved for
review. There is no recommendation to tune constants, change a gate or implement
RSO4. No acceptance is claimed.

## 8. Independent final confirmation

The independent reviewer returned **CONFIRM REJECTION**. It verified C=299,703 B,
excess 37,559 B, and the reduced C=268,471 B, excess 6,327 B, when all deltas and
signed ranges are removed. It confirmed that pending generations are required for
crash-safe four-copy availability and that current catalogs, deltas, manifests and
delegations cannot simply be removed. F and aggregate storage cannot rescue per-
owner C. Ephemeral rather than durable signed ranges would not change the rejection.

The reviewer also corrected the displayed per-scope delta work line from 231,264 to
**216,864 B**. The additional-work total 4,396,860 B and cumulative 265,481,078 B
already used the correct value and therefore remain unchanged. Its 2,954,378 B
cumulative margin is not acceptance headroom or a physical remedy. No implementation,
threshold change, campaign or R1/R2/K-gate acceptance is justified.
