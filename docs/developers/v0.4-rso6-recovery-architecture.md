# RSO6 incremental recovery architecture — DESIGN REJECTED

**Status: DEC-005 design-only review, REJECTED before implementation.** R1/R2 and all
K1–K8 gates remain open. This is not production routing, implementation authorization,
a 100-transition fit claim, timing evidence or an acceptance result. No cap, trust,
physical, deadline or business-semantics change is proposed.

## 1. Decision inputs and scope

DEC-005 authorizes one incremental recovery design and bottom-up forecast. Inputs were
the RSO5 cost analysis, implementation receipt and source, RSO5 specification and the
RSO4 mixed-generation rejection. No production or product-test files were edited and no
campaign, Git/CI or installer action occurred.

The model preserves:

- exact original business objects, signatures and reducers, accepted rights, permanent
  denials, conflicts and uncertainty;
- five pinned members, four votes, at most one Byzantine participant;
- original C/L/D/P/F, per-operation W=4,718,592, cumulative 268,435,456 B,
  aggregate 2 GiB/512 reservation entries/1 MiB metadata and 3 s/30 s limits;
- complete prospective funding before any reservation, freeze, readiness or external
  completion promise;
- role-private proof recovery with no predecessor service and no replica plaintext.

The forecast uses actual RSO5 records and named work multipliers. It is not a claim of
measured RSO6 savings. There is no percentage-based combined-savings estimate.

## 2. Core incremental model

### 2.1 Immutable proof and business roots

RSO5 immutable frames, scope IDs, per-proof DEKs, scope-key versions, recipient HPKE
enrollments and historical original signatures are retained without changing their
signed domains. For each selected proof, the exact reference remains:

```
scopeId || proofId || kind || frameDigest || frameBytes
```

The current business proof root is the canonical digest of the sorted complete selected
reference set, not a snapshot version. A delta additionally binds the previous and new
business state roots. Orphan frames may consume declared C but cannot enter that set or
influence business state.

Configuration is authenticated separately for every delta and cut. A signer must belong
to the executed configuration active at that sequence/activation boundary, not merely
to the union of old, new and historical sets. Returning history may contain original and
disjoint successor configurations.

### 2.2 `RSO6J001` signed control delta

A control delta contains:

```
magic[8] || version[4] || sequence[8] || view[4]
previousRoot[32] || currentRoot[32] || configuration[32]
commandDigest[32] || evidenceRoot[32] || flags[4]
preparedReference[32] || committedReference[32] || stableReference[32]
signature[64]
```

Absent optional references use the specified zero digest. The fixed body is 348 B;
the authenticated archive header is 256 B, so one stored delta is **604 B**. Deltas are
append-only and linked by exact previous/current roots. Four matching deltas are
required before the corresponding journal operation completes.

A delta commits only control references and changed evidence. It never substitutes for
an original vote, certificate, policy, activation or command frame.

### 2.3 `RSO6S001` cut snapshot

A cut snapshot contains the complete binary RBJ1 business state, selected proof
references, configuration, policy/activation heads, accepted/denied state and prepared/
locked/stable floors. Its signed record has a 64-byte signature and 256-byte archive
header. The sealed state image remains bounded by the existing J=65,536 state limit;
with signature/header its physical reservation is **65,856 B** and remains outside C.

Snapshots are created only at stable cuts. At most 32 control deltas follow a snapshot.
A current and pending snapshot may coexist only during funded replacement. This is not
an untracked pending catalog: snapshot, delta, delegation and head records all have
explicit L/D/P reservations and work charges.

## 3. Crash-safe replacement and funding

Before every effect, preflight the complete current and pending generations, delegation
restoration, all frames, snapshots, deltas, pending heads and failed extents. Every
snapshot pending generation is authenticated and fsynced before a commit is possible.

| Matching complete pending copies | Required state/selection |
| ---: | --- |
| 0 | Previous snapshot and suffix remain complete and authoritative. |
| 1 | Previous generation remains authoritative; one pending generation is only pending. |
| 3 | Previous generation remains authoritative; three pending copies are only pending. |
| 4 | Four complete pending copies permit local commit; previous generation is retained through that commit. |
| Local commit | New complete generation is selected; old records may retire only after four new complete copies and local commit. |

The previous and new complete states therefore remain available at 0/1/3/4 pending
copies and local commit. Torn pending data cannot damage the previous generation. A
pending snapshot/delegation/head is never free or implicitly funded.

This is mandatory to avoid RSO4's local-last/mixed-generation failure. Compaction cannot
remove a prepared lock, committed floor, stable cut, required delegation or generation
needed by the selected proof root. Rotation or replacement refuses before effects if
complete funding fails.

F remains 32,768 B. Complete failed extents are retained where they fit. If the failed
extent exceeds F, recovery from that tail is unavailable and repair/replacement refuses
before modifying the survivor; previous complete state is not sacrificed. No partial
record becomes available merely because its prefix is valid.

## 4. Signed-bytes transport and exact replay

`RSO5F001`, `RSO5D001`, `RSO5H001`, `RSO6J001` and `RSO6S001` use strict binary
version, big-endian lengths, canonical field order, exact terminal lengths and no
trailing data. Signed bytes are transported unchanged; there is no JSON/base64 state or
reply projection. Replicas authenticate digest, scope, configuration, sequence and
identity before accepting payload.

Transport charging still includes every real binary parse, input/output buffer, copy,
signature, verification, encryption, decryption, disk read/write and four-copy
replication. Binary transport is not a claim of zero copies or zero signature work.
Same-record replay is idempotent; same-revision/different-record is a conflict.

For frames, delegations, checkpoints and deltas, replicas first return exact status.
Payload is fetched only when required. Replies are grouped by requested digest rather
than first arrival. Exact duplicate checkpoint replay does not append another record.

## 5. Incremental evidence and transition construction

Authenticated indexes map:

- `(issuerKey, spend)` to exact vote/certificate proof references;
- configuration and activation IDs to exact policy/activation references;
- proof references to their original scope/signer chain.

An index is only a locator. Original signatures and full original reducers remain the
business authority. Startup or an unknown index entry requires the corresponding full
reducer path. Incremental and full evaluation must agree for every conflict, denial,
split uncertainty, retry and restart, including current-set requests against old-epoch
proofs.

`nextState()` runs once per command. In the measured RSO5 scenario that is 24
evaluations rather than 120 (24 proposals, 48 prepares and 48 executes), eliminating 96
evaluations. No savings number is inferred from that count alone.

Size-only preflight computes exact binary field lengths and all required state/pending
images without four speculative journal encodings. It must equal the actual encoded
length and include every frame, delta, snapshot, delegation and failed extent before
reservation.

## 6. Recovery and transfer

Recovery uses four exact statuses and one payload. A conflicting or missing payload
causes exact digest-race retry; a fast conflicting replica cannot win. Frames are
verified for requested scope/proof/kind/length/digest before local append.

Durable delegation restoration validates exporter, recipient, wrapping key, scope and
key version before any write. Existing exact delegations are replayed idempotently and
their scope keys are opened once and reused for all selected proofs. Restoration is not
free: validation, writes and retained bytes are charged and preflighted within the
3,549 B C headroom. Only the three existing delegation identities are restored; no new
delegation is silently admitted.

Cold transfer includes every selected frame, snapshot/suffix and delegation. Returning
and new-view transfer sends only missing frames/suffix/delegations after comparing exact
business roots and checkpoint heads. Four matching copies remain mandatory even when a
payload is already retained. Imported original scope/signatures and historical signer
chains are checked before reservation.

After predecessor shutdown, recipients use their own enrolled P-256 material to unwrap
scope keys and then per-proof DEKs. Replicas see only ciphertext and signed metadata.
No predecessor storage, replica plaintext or new original-owner countersignature is
required.

## 7. Physical closure

The selected inventory is 72 proofs/215,344 B across up to ten scopes. C contains only
immutable frames, their archive headers and the three existing delegation bundles.
Snapshots, deltas, policy images and pending heads are outside C.

```
frame contents = 215344 + 72*265             = 234,424
archive headers = 72*256                     =  18,432
delegations    = 3*(553 + 2*68*10)           =   5,739
                                                 ---------
C total                                         258,595
C limit                                         262,144
headroom                                          3,549
```

Durable restoration uses those exact existing bytes and adds no new C identity or
payload. A fourth delegation/rotation identity would require another funded model; it
cannot be hidden in the 3,549 B headroom.

Snapshot+delta D reservation:

```
current + pending snapshots = 2*(65536 + 64 + 256) = 131,712
32 control deltas          = 32*604                =  19,328
                                                   ---------
D total                                            = 151,040
D limit                                            = 524,288
```

L/P=65,536 each covers one sealed state image or pending head. Complete prospective
funding covers these partitions before reservation. Existing aggregate accounting is
unchanged: 2,042,919,814 B / 444 entries, leaving 104,563,834 B and 68 entries. No new
reservation component is used to evade per-owner C.

Physical C closes. This does not establish work closure or 100-transition physical fit.

## 10. Verdict and blockers

**RSO6 is REJECTED before implementation.**

- First cold reserve is **4,789,307 B**, **70,715 B over W=4,718,592**.
- Listed cumulative rows total **311,198,374 B**, **42,762,918 B over** 268,435,456 B;
  this is optimistic and incomplete, not a complete total.
- Physical C=258,595 B and D=151,040 B close, but they do not rescue per-operation or
  cumulative work.
- Mandatory current+pending snapshots are real work, not free transient state. Removing
  them would recreate RSO4's availability failure rather than produce a valid candidate.
- Delegation restoration adds validation, writes and retained bytes and must fit the
  3,549 B C headroom. New rotations/delegations are not funded by this manifest.

No tuning of constants, removal of pending generations, per-participant reset or hidden
copy is permitted to reverse this verdict.

Independent review **CONFIRMS REJECTION**: C=258,595 B, D=151,040 B, first cold reserve
4,789,307 B (70,715 B over W), and listed cumulative rows 311,198,374 B (42,762,918 B
over the cumulative comparison). The view-change per-operation value is S+11Δ=599,592;
the cumulative view-change row was already correct. The review also confirms that the
cumulative total is optimistic/incomplete and cannot be used as an implementation GO.

## 11. Falsifier for a later separate owner decision

A future separately authorized implementation candidate would have to demonstrate, on a
complete up-to-ten-scope/72-proof cold bootstrap and two-transition lifecycle:

1. C<=262,144, per-operation W<=4,718,592 and cumulative<=268,435,456 with no cap change
   or omitted mandatory work.
2. Exact previous/current state roots and four-copy availability for every delta and
   snapshot at 0/1/3/4 pending copies and local commit.
3. No lost prepared lock, committed floor, stable cut or required generation during
   snapshot/compaction and F exhaustion.
4. Exact original business outcomes for every conflict, denial, split, retry and restart
   using both incremental and full-original reducers.
5. Signed-bytes canonicality, same-record replay, same-revision conflicts, orphan-frame
   non-effect, authorized historical signers and digest-race substitution resistance.
6. Cold, returning and new-view convergence with mixed original/disjoint configurations,
   predecessor shutdown and role-private recovery without replica plaintext.
7. Delegation restoration and scope-key reuse within C headroom, including a fourth
   identity refusal before effects.
8. Complete prospective funding, reservation and failed-extent matrices, then separate
   real 3 s/30 s maximum-envelope timing and energy measurements.

This falsifier is not authorization to implement RSO6 and is not evidence that the model
fits. Independent review of the rejection and arithmetic is still required.
