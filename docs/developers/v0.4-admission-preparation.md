# Preparing fresh policy evidence while awaiting a decision

## Motivation

Bounded policy batches and atomic certificate/spend persistence reduce repeated
work, but the 25 ms whole-application measurement still accepts only three of
six paired searches. The protected owner serializes the complete transaction,
including its initial read-only policy query. That query can be prepared while
the previous transaction is doing its witness and history writes.

Simply moving the existing freshness wrapper outside the decision queue is
unsafe for availability: it holds one of two freshness slots through its whole
callback. Two coordinator requests could occupy both and prevent the same
relay's witness from obtaining the observation needed to finish the first request.

## Selected scheduling boundary

1. After token verification, admit the job to the existing eight-caller queue and
   establish its original three-second controller and monotonic/wall deadlines.
2. A separate FIFO preparation lane allows at most one coordinator policy
   collection at a time. It may overlap a preceding job's serialized transaction.
   Preparation exposes separate `ready` and `drained` promises: four authenticated
   replies can make evidence usable while an aborted fifth adapter remains charged.
3. Each preparation performs its own signed, challenged quorum observation and
   preserves any observed policy conflict in the existing store. It returns a
   validator for that caller's evidence; it never supplies authority to a later
   caller.
4. The writer lane stays FIFO. Its job awaits its own preparation and checks the
   original queue lifetime before reading protected history or doing durable
   work. Witness votes, authorization and spend persistence remain inside it.
5. Before and after the final write, both the original job and policy evidence
   must remain valid. Preparation does not reset or extend either deadline.

The detached validator retains the original query expiry, monotonic deadline,
installed head and external cancellation signal. Every use checks those facts,
closed state, current policy and the durable observed/conflict floor. The
temporary collection controller can be cleaned up without treating its deliberate
abort as expiry of still-valid evidence. The validator adds no timer or socket.

## Resource and cancellation invariants

The existing two-slot policy responder is shared with witness observations.
Only one coordinator preparation can compete with that witness path. The
original `run()` freshness operation continues holding its slot through its
callback. A prepared validator is retained by its already-counted decision job;
there can be no more than eight such queued/active jobs.

Cancellation may return promptly to a caller, but a blocked preparation remains
charged until it actually settles. A valid four-reply decision may also finish
while the fifth adapter is still draining; the finished job remains counted
until that cleanup actually settles. Its remaining adapter cannot prevent the
first valid decision, and cannot free a slot for unbounded further preparations.
The next coordinator collection waits for both readiness and drainage to settle.
In particular, a job that expires before its writer turn cannot release its slot
while an abort-ignoring preparation is still running. Shutdown drains both lanes.
No late result starts a write, and invalid tokens never enter either lane or cause
observer fan-out.

## Valid conflicting-token preflight

Preparation follows cryptographic token verification but precedes the serialized
spend-history lookup. A correctly signed token with an already-used, conflicting
request binding therefore performs a fresh bounded policy query before its
conflict is found. It still causes no witness fan-out or durable mutation. Moving
the lookup outside the writer would race accepted history updates. Invalid tokens
continue to cause no policy query, witness request or queue admission.

## Validation status

Independent reviews approved the queue and freshness integration. Twenty queue
tests and eighteen freshness/pipeline tests pass, including preparation overlap
with FIFO writes, original expiry under changed clocks, eight-caller and
two-collection bounds, cancellation with ignored aborts, four-reply readiness
before fifth-transport drainage, distinct fresh challenges, policy changes and
conflicts, and two configured decisions when the coordinator is a required
witness. Twenty-five existing role/atomic-commit cases pass after updating the
two query-count assertions for valid conflicting-token preflight; explicit
zero-work assertions cover invalid tokens and durable conflicting retries.

The composed TypeScript build and strict standalone evaluator typecheck pass.
The subsequent full application measurement still accepts only three of six
paired searches at 25 ms one-way delay; all eight other checkpoints pass. See
[the complete negative result](../evals/v04-prepared-admission-2026-10-18.md).
No deadline, quorum or resource cap has been increased.
