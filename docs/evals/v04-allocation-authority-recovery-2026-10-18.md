# Allocation-authority owner-loss recovery experiment

Parent **B-HISTORY-LIFECYCLE**, contributing to K2/K4. This is the next semantic
application of the existing freeze/agreement/download/provision protocol. It
does not reset cumulative alternatives or effort and introduces no eligibility
or trust assumption. The protected authority commit API is the prerequisite.

## Predeclared hypothesis and acceptance

Given independently retained authority/history keys, original enrollment and
complete selected volunteer ancestry, a reviewed successor owner can reconstruct
every signed plan and claim reservation without replacement, reassignment or
quota refund. An available original owner must complete exact pending evidence
and become durably fenced; an unavailable owner is not a license to ignore known
evidence. Restoration itself never grants live readiness.

Minimum meaningful improvement: two complete recoveries using five actual local
volunteer services, the second after deleting owner/writer/archive. Semantic
replay must happen before agreement confirmation, authenticate the original empty
v2 genesis and every continuation, preserve exact signatures and reject omitted
known local evidence. Reopened successor state requires four fresh confirmations;
three refuse. Historical expiry is preserved, with no new expired decisions.

Reject validly signed semantic violations: replaced or reordered plans, removed
or reassigned claims, combined appends, wrong role/genesis, invalid capacity or
allocation linkage. Reject false/cloned review capabilities, wrong destinations,
symlink retargeting and raw baseline/validator/domain overrides. Bound replay
blocks, bytes, generations and time using the existing issuer limits. Preserve
64-plan/2,048-assignment limits and archive capacity; no compaction is enabled.

Sol A owns authority semantic replay and WeakMap-reviewed preparation; Sol B owns
fixed-role restoration and source-fence integration. The coordinator owns actual
volunteer recovery integration and documentation. Astra reviews composition.
API-only recovery is this phase's scope; resumable operator orchestration and
interrupted pristine restoration completion require their own subsequent tests.

Resources: one local computer, finite maintenance attempts, independently retained
keys and pinned member roles. No new cryptography, production service, merge or
installer. A structural counterexample or reproducible safety violation invokes
the agreed stop policy; unavailable external qualification is not replaced by
local results. This phase alone cannot close K2 or K4.
