### Independent gate-versus-architecture verdict

The owner requested this reassessment after the diagnostic evidence. The reviewer
recommends **a fundamentally different recovery-storage architecture; do not revise
the resource gates from this evidence**. Neither immutable-binary nor RSO3 provides
a viable contract-change basis: the former lacks predecessor-independent recovery;
the latter adds that property but fails both work and physical C.

The failures are independent. Bootstrap is 2.11× and first reserveInstall 1.98× the
unchanged per-operation work cap, with several other operation families also over it.
Physical C separately requires 270,204 against 262,144 before the returning lifecycle.
Increasing W cannot repair C; increasing C cannot repair W and can increase later
validation/transfer/deadline work. Per-recipient capsule growth across transitions
is structural, so the observed 8,060-byte C excess is not a lifetime envelope.

Current RSO3 is not eligible for a complete contract-change diagnostic. Returning
cut/phases, second transfer/reserveInstall/install/startup/reopen/recovery and longer

complete retained-obligation manifest; derived per-owner and aggregate 2 GiB/512/

must depend on selected state/new obligations rather than the entire historical set.
