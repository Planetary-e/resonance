# Allocation-authority recovery operator experiment

Parent **B-HISTORY-LIFECYCLE**, contributing to K2/K4. This applies the existing
protected-history recovery protocol to allocation authority operation. It does
not reset cumulative effort or alternatives, change eligibility or establish a
complete KPI.

## Predeclared acceptance

Two gates are required. First, mechanically extract the common recovery and
pristine restoration engines while preserving the existing issuer's public
entrypoints, command grammar, encrypted journal format, review serialization and
approval digests. The independent old producer is the complete `04c86f6` source
snapshot, with its own workspace package resolution. Old persisted artifacts must
remain readable without migration, including intermediate phases; equivalent
reviews must accept the previously computed digest. Read-only inspection must not
rewrite retained journals. Existing issuer CLI and interruption regressions remain
mandatory. A format or semantic change stops this extraction for reassessment.

Second, add fixed-role allocation-authority recovery and explicit operator
commands. No caller may inject a role, parser, persistence domain or restoration
baseline. Capture independent keys, original genesis, canonical paths, exact
available-source evidence, members/endpoints and effective protocol/replay/archive
bounds before asynchronous work. Unavailable source is an explicit declaration.
Authenticate selected business history before agreement confirmation. Recovery
and restoration completion never imply readiness or start a service.

The meaningful improvement is two actual CLI recovery generations preserving
signed allocations, assignments and consumed capacity, with independently retained
keys and five local volunteer services. Process-death cases cover source fencing,
workspace creation, every component's creation/registration, confirmed/provisioned
boundaries and restored owner/writer/archive initialization. Resume exact durable
work or refuse with evidence preserved. Three replicas must refuse readiness;
four current confirmations are required. Historical expired signatures remain
exportable offline; new expired decisions, reassignment and quota refunds refuse.

Pristine completion requires the authenticated restored owner and independently
replayed exact baseline. Missing committed history, later suffixes, unknown files,
tampering, path retargeting and changed approvals cannot authorize recreation.
Test cancellation, managed storage authority, exclusive private outputs and private
claim input permissions. Preserve all existing storage and generation bounds.

Sol A owns internal resumption extraction and fixed wrappers. Sol B owns recovery
extraction, fixed wrappers and authority commands. The coordinator owns independent
compatibility evidence and actual-process integration. Astra reviews composition.
No production activation, merge, release or native installer is included.

The existing agreed stop conditions apply. Unknown historical effort is not
reconstructed; this experiment cannot close K2/K4, supply independent eligibility
labels, cross the history-generation capacity boundary or replace the K8 pilot.
